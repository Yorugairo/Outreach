# Full-Episode Evidence Coverage And Generation

## Summary

Turn the approved deck corpus into an episode-wide, cue-bound evidence plan
for `current-bubble-mechanism`. The current world-plate library is strong and
composition-approved; P32 does not reopen its aesthetic approval. The edit
must instead rotate to a relevant new scene at least every 20 seconds, while
each evidence surface remains a small, readable, source-bound object that
enters through a safe slot, is drawn in one at a time, and retracts before an
unrelated fact enters.

The baseline makes the gap concrete: the 980.806-second episode has 290 cues,
95 approved deck/evidence assets, and a 76-plate visual library (36 current
mapped world/mechanism plates plus 40 composition-approved sentence-native
plates), but only two current semantic-binding records (one recommended and
one unmatched). P32 closes that coverage and context gap. It does not lower
factual, provenance, rights, or approval standards.

## Intent And Acceptance

- Every one of the 11 episode scenes has a reviewable evidence-coverage matrix
  bound to canonical cue IDs, claim IDs, world-plate IDs, and source hashes.
- No world plate may be held longer than 20 seconds without a relevant new
  scene. The 60-segment animatic/semantic map is the starting point, but its
  14 over-20-second holds must be broken with the approved sentence-native
  plates before any new world art is requested.
- Early explanation beats receive denser support: the first three minutes must
  include a source-backed visual whenever the narration introduces a material
  mechanism, measured comparison, capacity constraint, or customer commitment.
- A world plate may host up to three evidence artifacts sequentially and two
  only for a direct comparison; no more than two may occupy one horizontal row.
- Existing approved PowerPoint material is selected and cropped before any new
  visual is proposed. **Superseded by operator decision 2026-08-15:** preserve
  each teacher-stamped slide intact; do not crop away or partially obscure the
  teacher stamp. A generated visual may explain a source-bound mechanism only
  if it is an immediately recognizable analogy/caricature and is separately
  approved; it is never literal proof or a replacement for a cited
  metric/table/source excerpt.
- The editor exposes all 36 mapped world/mechanism plates as **World plates**,
  plus 40 composition-approved sentence-native plates, separated from approved
  factual evidence. They stay visual-only unless a separate evidence contract
  says otherwise.
- The compiler produces recommendations for every eligible cue, records
  `recommended`, `unmatched`, or `manual_only`, and fails closed for weak or
  ambiguous matches. It never inserts evidence automatically.
- The final review packet groups coverage by scene and includes a mobile
  legibility contact sheet, cue/claim/source provenance, candidate status, and
  normal/diagnostic renders. No gate authorizes publication.

## Scope

- A versioned full-episode coverage matrix and sparse-evidence proposal
  register derived from canonical cue, beat, claim, plate, deck, and approval
  artifacts.
- Expansion of semantic-crop context for already approved deck visuals, then
  deterministic matching across all canonical cues.
- A cadence-aware scene map made from the existing `edit` semantic resolution
  map, the full-review animatic, and the composition-approved plate library;
  it may request new art only after those assets cannot cover a relevant
  sub-20-second turn.
- Reviewed layout profiles/slots for the current plate map, including mobile
  safe geometry and the world/evidence/caption hierarchy.
- A gated production path for newly needed evidence: existing source crop,
  deterministic local evidence card, source-bound generated explanatory
  illustration, or explicitly rejected/no-evidence outcome.
- Editor, renderer, and review packet integration using the existing P30/P31
  immutable snapshot/revision contracts.

## Not Building

- No narration, transcript, word-timing, claim-ledger, or source-deck rewrite.
- No automatic evidence insertion, automatic fact approval, or conversion of
  generated illustrations into literal evidence.
- No full-slide takeover, hero-plate replacement, or persistent evidence
  collage; evidence is subordinate to the plate and absent during transitions.
- No arbitrary live model/embedding/browser dependency in the editor.
- No publication, investment recommendation, or new performance claim.

### Evidence Status Taxonomy

1. **approved_existing** — approved stamped deck visual or semantic crop that
   already supports the cue and has source/hash context.
2. **existing_context_needed** — approved slide exists but needs a reviewed
   crop, claim/cue tags, and negative-space placement.
3. **source_pack_needed** — the claim ledger/research identifies a fact but
   the visual corpus lacks a readable primary-source crop or deterministic
   card specification.
4. **illustration_candidate** — a non-literal, generated mechanism visual
   carrying no unverified factual text; it must point to a separately approved
   source card if the narration makes a factual claim.
5. **manual_only / no_evidence** — no safe or sufficiently supported match;
   the world plate and canonical caption continue without a speculative card.

## Execution Path

1. Compile an immutable cadence and evidence baseline from the canonical
   episode, `edit/semantic-v2` resolution map, and full-review animatic; do
   not infer coverage from filenames or edit drafts.
2. Map each cue to its active world plate, candidate evidence slot, claim set,
   current source candidates, and one of the five evidence statuses above.
3. Split all existing plate holds longer than 20 seconds with a relevant
   composition-approved plate already present in the sentence-native/animatic
   library. Only unresolved turns become new-world-art candidates.
4. Prioritize `existing_context_needed` candidates from the approved decks;
   derive reviewed crops and context before requesting new art.
5. For genuine gaps, create a source-pack-backed proposal that states exact
   claims, data/date/qualifier, permitted factual text, plate/slot, and whether
   the requested asset is literal evidence, deterministic card, or explanatory
   illustration.
6. After Gates A–C, register promoted assets with hashes/provenance, compile
   deterministic ranked recommendations, and keep failures `unmatched`.
7. Produce group review sheets, accept selected bindings explicitly in editor
   revisions, and render the same revision through Player and deterministic
   output for Gate E.
