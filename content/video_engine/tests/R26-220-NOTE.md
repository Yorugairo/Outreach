## The change

### 1. THE DIAL — `focus_zoom` takes a `zoom`

`{"kind": "focus_zoom", "at": …, "dur": …, "zoom": 1.08, "target": {…}}`. **Absent = the engine's own
1.32**, so every timeline on disk — every golden, both approved shorts, the H unit — renders the bytes it
rendered. Three places carry it and no module was edited:

- the engine's legacy `camXf` (`samples/scene-evidence-engine.mjs:12749`) and its kinetics path
  (`:12780`, `camSpeciesState(…, spCamP(sp))`): the helpers are `spZoom` / `spCamP` at `:12668-12669` (and `CAP_YIELD` at `:12661`).
  **The module's own 5th argument (`camSpeciesState(kind, k, c, dur, P = CAM)`, `kinetics/camera.mjs:58`)
  IS the per-window dial door** — a per-row value is the author's, resolved by the caller exactly as
  `sp.dur` is — so `kinetics/camera.mjs` is untouched and `sync_kinetics.py --check` reads *in sync
  (42 module(s))* before and after. Both paths were RENDERED and measured equal (the test below).
- the compiler's shape check (`build_scene_timeline_f.py:1897`): a number `> 1.0`, refused by name;
  `zoom` on a `punch` or a `pull_back` is refused too (*"a dial that does nothing is worse than no dial"*).

### 2. THE REACHABLE ZOOM — the compiler refuses what the page cannot take, by name and with the number

`build_scene_timeline_f.py:661-839`: `page_glyph_boxes`, `zoom_ceiling`, `page_zoom_ceiling`,
`page_crop_line_ceiling`, `camera_zoom_errors`; called at the row in `:6378`, after `world_for_plate` has
read the page (the ceiling is the PAGE's, so it cannot be checked beside `validate_species`).

**THE LAW is arithmetic, not taste.** E99 s80 (2) asks for each element *"fully in or fully out of the
stage at every instant"*. A zoom is continuous, so an element that starts on the stage cannot reach "fully
out" without straddling the edge on the way — **"at every instant" reduces to every GLYPH wholly on the
stage at the move's deepest zoom**. What may be cropped is the page's FIELD (the cream, the board, the
chart's own ink), which is the thing a punch moves into. The camera's own similarity gives the ceiling per
element and per edge:

```
screen = at + s * (p - look)                       (kinetics/camera.mjs; at == look is a zoom in place)
left   s <= at_x / (look_x - box.x)      right   s <= (W - at_x) / (box.right - look_x)
top    s <= at_y / (look_y - box.y)      bottom  s <= (H - at_y) / (box.bottom - look_y)
```

The glyphs are the ones the ruling names: `title`, `sub`, `source` (the cite), `rail` (the pill row),
`tags` (the inline end names), plus the two label banks cut back out of the `plot` box by the box model's
own constants — the **y tick column** (`chart.x` → `plot.x`) and the **x tick labels** (`LPG.XTICK_H` at
the plot's foot). A glyph already off the stage at rest does not bind: the dense-line page's ESTIMATED tag
column runs past the frame by construction (`LAND_TAG_REACH` is an upper bound; the frame measures the
widest tag ending at 1896 of 1920), and what the page already does with no camera is R26-205's row.

**At 16:9 a second ceiling**, because the caption there is already anchored and cannot yield:
`page_crop_line_ceiling` refuses a zoom that walks the page's own bottom edge INTO `CAPTION_ANCHOR` — a
deckle edge cutting the caption's band. It is silent for a full-stage page (whose ink already runs past
the strip's foot, which is why the H unit's refusal is a glyph's) and silent at 9:16 (where the caption
moves instead).

### 3. THE YIELD — the caption moves under a camera move as it moves under a card

`build_scene_timeline_f.py:5535-5702`: `CAPTION_YIELD_KEY`, `CAMERA_SPECIES_ZOOM`, `camera_move_windows`,
`camera_frame_xf`, `camera_caption_band`, `caption_live_box`, `caption_strip_intrusion`,
`page_caption_holds_the_stage`, `stamp_camera_caption_bands`; stamped in the build at `:6662` beside
E62's own dock pass, and read by the engine at `samples/scene-evidence-engine.mjs:16172-16183`.

**The mechanism is E62's, unchanged** — the below / above / quiet ladder off `free_bands` / `page_boxes`,
the strip two lines at the stage size, the first candidate that holds clear wins, `null` meaning "keep the
anchor". ONE thing is new: the page's ink is read **where the frame puts it**. `caption_band` takes an
optional `xf` (`:5314`, the camera's similarity, rounded to whole pixels as `ledger_page._box` is — a
fractional box puts the flush candidate 0.4 px into the plot and drops the caption to the anchor for a
pixel nobody can see, measured while this row was built). The band is cut at the move's DEEPEST zoom and
held for the whole window: a band that tracked the zoom would be a caption sliding under a camera, which
is what E62 already refused (*"a layout move on the dock's enter clock, no transition"*).

Who yields: a caption that **holds the stage**. A 16:9 page row's caption is already in the anchored strip
(R26-205) and a page that declares `caption: "anchor"` itself (a host plate, C5) has no band to take — for
those the rule is the page's crop line above. In practice this is the 9:16 page's stage caption, which is
exactly the frame R26-201 was measured on.

**Where no band holds, the number is named.** A 9:16 page whose chart fills the frame leaves 44 px under

page's own cite is pushed into it. The window then carries `in_strip: {element, px}` and the build prints

```

(zoom 1.08) at 2.0s: the source 27px in; … . The caption has no band to yield to on this page: ANCHOR the
move (a key's `at`, lifting the look until the foot clears) or PARK the page (E61) - or the move stays out.
```

which is the ruling's own fallback (s80 (3): *"where the engine has no door for that, the punch anchors"*)
and is what v3b did by hand. Run over Tokyo's four page scenes with a 1.08 key pair: **s04 yields to the
`above` band at y 491; s01, s03 and s05 report the cite 27 / 27 / 21 px in with the two ways out.** The
engine gap is closed; which pages can carry a punch is now an authoring decision with numbers on it.

## What I could not do, with a path:line

- **the `page-punch-16x9` golden pair.** A new surface needs an entry in `FRAME_T` and a builder in
  `content/video_engine/tests/golden/build_golden_sources.py` **and** a name in
  `content/video_engine/tests/test_golden_frames.py:27` (`SURFACES`, read by
  `render_baseline.load_surface` → `FRAME_T[name]`, `render_baseline.py:506`). `test_golden_frames.py` is
  not in this lane's write set, and the register is what makes a golden a golden. There is a second
  reason not to sneak it in: `build_golden_sources.py --write` would now stamp `full_stage` on every 16:9
  page surface it builds through `world_for_plate` (R26-205-NOTE.md, "NO GOLDEN MOVED, at either
  aspect"), so adding a 16:9 page surface is the same reviewable step R26-205 deliberately deferred. The
  two rendered 16:9 probes in this file read the same frames by BOX rather than by pixel; the pair is
  owed with R26-205's re-render.
- **the ceiling at 16:9 carries R26-27's y error, measured at 5 px.** A full-stage page is never served
  the measured fixture (`ledger_page.measured_entry`, the R26-205 refusal at `ledger_page.py:1547`), so
  its ceiling is the estimate's: the estimate puts this page's title at y 44 and the frame draws it at
  39.2, which is why the reachable 1.08 still leaves the title's top 1.3 px off the frame. The rendered
  test measures that error rather than assuming it and allows exactly that much. `measure_page_boxes.py
  --write` closes it and `content/video_engine/assets/page-boxes.v1.json` is outside this write set — the
  same sentence R26-205-NOTE.md ends on.
- **a DATUM's look is a PROXY at compile time.** `MG._cam_point` resolves a datum to the plot's centre
  (`gate_motion_density.py:1013`), and the engine resolves it to the mark itself: v3b measured the two
  450 px apart on a line page (`build_short_v3.py:241`). So a refusal on a datum target is measured
  against the page's own centre and the message prints the anchor it read. The exact answer can only come
  off a frame (`window.__camera(t, target)`), which is a gate's reading, not a compile-time one — the
  rendered tests use POINT targets for that reason. A row that wants the engine's own number still has to
  build once and read it, as v3b did.
- **the newsreel band is not consulted by the yield.** `newsreel_caption_band` post-processes a DOCK's
  band (`build_scene_timeline_f.py:5449`) and a yielded band could in principle land on a live crawl; no
  build pairs a camera move with a crawl on one row, and the strip law already refuses the pair at the
  caption's home (`validate_newsreel_strip`). Named, not fixed.
- **no project file was touched** (the brief forbids it): the H unit's 1.06 keys, Tokyo's dropped
  punches and `build_episode_h.py` are as they were. Turning the H unit's keys back into a `focus_zoom`
  at 1.08, and Tokyo's punches back on with an anchor, are frame decisions for T6 and for the short's own
  lane.
