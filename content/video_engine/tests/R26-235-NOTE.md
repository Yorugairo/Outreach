## The change

**THE KEY IS THE GEOMETRY, AND THE GEOMETRY IS WHERE THE ASPECT ALREADY WAS.** A page's boxes are a
function of its INK (`page_ink_key`) and of the geometry that ink is drawn in. The fixture's second
key has always been the geometry — the aspect — so the flag goes THERE:

- `ledger_page.FULL_STAGE_SUFFIX` / `box_key(spec, aspect)` (`ledger_page.py:1488-1495`) — `16:9`,
  `16:9|full_stage`, `9:16`. Every entry measured before this row still answers for the page it
  measured, and `page_ink_key` is untouched, which is the half R26-205 reverted the hard way (the flag
  in the INK key re-keyed every 9:16 page a caller stamped with the compiler's `ASPECT` unset).
- `measured_entry` (`ledger_page.py:1553-1570`) — looks the entry up under `box_key` in both sections
  (ink-keyed `pages` first, then the builder's representative) and refuses an entry whose own
  `full_stage` flag disagrees with the key it is filed under. The refusal is gone; the guard is what
  replaces it, so a measurement can never be re-interpreted into the other geometry.
- `measure_page_boxes.full_stage_variant` / `variant_pages` (`measure_page_boxes.py:232-259`) — every
  16:9 page is measured twice, as declared and as the compiler stamps it. The stamp is
  `build_scene_timeline_f.stamp_full_stage` itself (with `ASPECT` pinned to `16:9` around the call and
  restored), so the fixture can only hold a geometry a build actually writes: a HOST PLATE is never
  stamped and keeps its single 16:9 entry, and a page that already carries the stamp (a 16:9 project's
  compiled page) measures once, under its own key.
- `entry` (`measure_page_boxes.py:354-366`) records `full_stage: True` on the entry it measured in that
  geometry; `build` and `build_pages` iterate the variants; the `--builder` print, the `--write` and the
  `--check` lines count GEOMETRIES rather than aspects.
- `assets/page-boxes.v1.json` re-measured: **5 new entries, and the 10 existing builder entries and
  Tokyo's 5 project pages byte-identical** (the diff is the new keys, the `measured` date and the
  `player_sha256`). The sha was STALE on this worktree — `43f21d50…` where the player now hashes
  `3660f540…`, from the engine work that has landed since 2026-09-18 — and every old box re-measured
  to the same pixel anyway, which is worth knowing: none of that engine work moved a page's layout.

## THE REACHABLE ZOOM DOES NOT MOVE, AND THAT IS THE FINDING

R26-220 expected this row to close its 1.3 px. It does not, and the number says why.

| | estimate | measured |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the glyph that binds | the **title's top edge** | the **title's top edge** |
| its box | `[67, 44, 1325, 63]` | `[67, 44, 1004, 63]` |
| the look (the plot's centre, the compiler's datum proxy) | `(624, 538)` | `(624.5, 541)` |
| the ceiling | **1.0891** | **1.0885** |
| the reachable zoom the author is handed | **1.08** | **1.08** |

**The estimate and the frame put this page's title at y 44 alike.** `_landscape_full_boxes`' y law is
the template's own, and this title wraps to one line in both, so there was no error to close. The 1.3
px R26-220 measured off a BUILT frame is the page's own **KEN BURNS** — `world.ken_burns.scale` 0.04,
no pan — which `page_boxes` models at neither aspect and which the fixture deliberately zeroes
(`measure_page_boxes._timeline`, `ken_burns {scale: 0, x: 0, y: 0}`: the fixture measures the layout,
not the push). Rendered here and pinned as ONE similarity about the stage's centre rather than as a
tolerance: at t = 20 s on the golden the scale is **1.0201**, and it carries the fixture's `title`,
`sub`, `chart` and `plot` onto the built frame within **2 px each**. The residual at the title's top
is `(540 - 44) * (scale - 1)` = **10.0 px** at that instant, 0 at the scene's start; R26-220 read 4.8
px of it (its own instant, scale ≈ 1.0097) and filed it as the box model's error. The camera ceiling
on a page with a ken is therefore still optimistic by up to `(cy - y) * ken.scale` — a compiler blind
spot with no row of its own, named at the end of this note.

The second ceiling is unchanged too: `page_crop_line_ceiling` stays silent on a full-stage page (its
ink already runs past the anchored strip's foot), measured `(inf, "-")`.
