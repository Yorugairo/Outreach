### The finding that decides the fix

**The 16:9 interior already moves MORE than the approved 9:16 interior** — 10.42 px against ~6.8 px on
the title, 3.60-8.31 px against ~2.05 px on the tick labels, over the same 2 s — and the operator reads
16:9 as dead and 9:16 as alive. So the gap is **not** the amplitude of the page's motion. It is:

1. **a rigid scale is not life.** Both aspects carry exactly one breath on the whole page and nothing
   else, so every word slides radially together: no word moves against its neighbour, no element has a
   phase of its own. The engine already knows how to do this and does it for pills (`20 + bi`) and for a
   layered plate's planes (each at its own k) — never for a page's interior.
2. **the row's `;idle=live` never reached the page at all.** The compiler writes it as `world["idle"]`
   (`build_scene_timeline_f.py:3824`, whose own docstring says *"the player reads it for the page or the
   plate; absent = the class default"*) and the engine's page painter read `world.page.idle`
   (`idleCssFor("page", pg.idle, ...)` - now `scene-evidence-engine.mjs:12487`, fixed). `page.idle` is
   **absent from every scene of both timelines** (unit s01/s03, Tokyo s01/s03/s04/s05), so
   `idleOf("page", undefined)` fell to `IDLE_CLASS.page = "breath"` and the drift half of `live` has never
   run on any page in any cut. The world-level pose cannot supply it either:
   `scene-evidence-engine.mjs:15501` hard-zeroes it for a ledger
   (`(isLedger || isClip || isVecmap) ? {scale: 1, dx: 0, dy: 0}`) on the stated ground that *"a ledger
   page ... carries its own motion"*, and the compiler refuses `;drift=` on a ledger row by name
   (`build_scene_timeline_f.py:3927`). The page's "own motion" was wired to a key nothing writes.
3. **the glow is 5.4x smaller as a share of the frame** at 16:9 (0.217 % against 1.181 %), because
   `LP_BLOOM_PX` is 6 user units at 16:9 and 12 at 9:16 while the 16:9 stage is 1.78x wider and its chart
   is drawn at ctm 0.69. On a phone: 0.85 CSS px against 4.60.
4. **the lead point is 1.5x smaller and gone for ~45 of the page's 54.9 s**, and it never sparked at
   either aspect.
5. **the type is under the phone floor at 16:9 on every page element** — R26-205's door, above.
