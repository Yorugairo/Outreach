## The token, and why that form

**`follow: <series name | series index>`** on the `chart_to rescale` species, or **`follow: true`** — the series
this row's own `build_to` draws. Three reasons for that spelling:

1. **It is a key on the SPECIES, not on the plate id.** `;domain=` (R26-223) and `;build=` (R26-226) are how the
   PAGE is born and how it draws at all; a follow is a property of ONE transition on ONE word, and the row may
   carry a second rescale that follows nothing. The species is where `ymin`, `ymax` and `window` already live.
2. **It names the LINE, not a clock.** The author is saying *which* line pushes the axis; the clock is then the
   line's own and there is nothing left to time. `follow: true` is the common case written once — the row has
   already named the series in its `build_to`, and a second spelling of it is a second place to be wrong.
3. **A NAME is legal because a page's series have names** (they are the end tags the viewer reads), and the
   compiler resolves the name to the index before the player ever sees it (`sp["follow"] = 3`) — so the engine
   reads one number and the timeline carries one truth.

A rescale that names none is exactly the rescale it was: the derived state, the species and every golden frame are
byte-identical (`test_a_rescale_that_names_no_follow_derives_exactly_what_it_always_did`, and the golden suite).

## The domain rule, per frame

Every frame, while the transition is on:

1. **What the line asks for.** The followed series' DRAWN extremum — the maximum value over the part of the line
   that is on the page — with the page's own air above it: `need = drawn x 1.06` (in log space, one offset of
   `log10(1.06)`). The drawn part is read off the path's own `stroke-dashoffset` **after** the standing chart is
   painted, so it is read off the ink and never off a clock (`lpFollowNeedY`, `:11694`; `lpDrawnMaxY`, `:11678`,
   walks the polyline in `capFrac`'s own length metric and interpolates the value inside the segment the nib is
   in, which is exact for a straight segment).
2. **Where that is on the A → B blend.** A rescale lerps the two MAPS, and a lerp of two affine maps is affine —
   but the domain it implies is not the lerp of the two domains. With `S = (1-u)/ra + u/rb` and
   `C = (1-u)y0a/ra + u.y0b/rb`, the blend is `y -> B - k(yS - C)`, whose bottom is `C/S` and whose range is
   `1/S` (`lpXfDomain`, `:11710`). So the top is `(1 + C)/S`, linear over linear in `u`, and it is **solved**:
   `u = (1 + C0 - need.S0) / (need.dS - dC)`, clamped to [0, 1] (`lpFollowU`, `:11721`). The pole lies outside
   [0, 1] for any two ranges, so on the window the top runs monotonically from A's to B's.
3. **The clamp IS the rule's two halves.** Under A's own top the answer is 0 — *nothing moves, never before the
   climb passes the old top*. Past B's top it is 1 — *the target stands, never after the line lands*. In between
   the domain's top **equals** the drawn extremum x 1.06, which is the table above (408.7 asked, 408.8 drawn;
   548.1 / 548.1; 622.0 / 622.2 — measured off the live ticks, not off the engine's own arithmetic).
4. **The ticks.** A standing tick fades only once the LIVE domain has left it behind — one air-band of travel
   (`XF_FOLLOW.AIR`, the same 6 %) — instead of fading against the TARGET domain on the transition's clock. A
   domain that is only opening never leaves one, so the low ticks stay lit and simply re-space: five ticks at
   every instant of the window, never under two.

## The finding this row turns on: what still draws under a rescale

`lpPaintRescale` paints the standing chart **fully built** (`lpPaintChart(A, 1, ...)`, `:11732`) — it has to: the
transition moves marks that exist. So the page's build fraction is 1 for the whole transition, and with it
R26-226's `;build=lines` turn. **What still moves under a rescale is the cap sequence**, which runs on absolute t
(`:11587`). And the FIRST cap by `at` is the level the build LANDS at (`f = f * capFrac(first)`, a constant while
c is 1), never a draw.

So a followed domain can only follow a line staged by `build_to` — the H unit's own shape — and the compiler says
exactly that when the row has not staged one (`follow_draw_windows`, `build_scene_timeline_f.py:3829`, reads the
caps AFTER the first; `test_the_first_build_to_is_the_level_the_build_lands_at_and_not_a_draw`).

## What I could not do, with a path:line

- **M23 will WARN on every followed rescale.** `gate_motion_density.py:2639` lists each `chart_to` and WARNs on a
  transition "over a build" (`:2650`, E45); a followed rescale is over the build BY CONSTRUCTION — that is the
  whole verb. The gate is outside this lane's write set. It needs one clause: a rescale carrying `follow` is on
  the line's own clock, and the line's clock IS the build.
- **`;build=lines`' turn does not survive a rescale**, so a follow must be staged with `build_to` caps
  (`scene-evidence-engine.mjs:11732` - the standing chart is painted fully built). Letting a page's build clock
  run under a transition is a different row and would move every existing rescale golden.
- **The drawn extremum is read on the CAP's own metric** — the polyline as the page was BUILT (`capFrac`,
  `scene-evidence-engine.mjs:10021`) — not on the re-projected path, so while the domain is mid-open the nib's
  true data position and the measured extremum differ by a second-order amount. Closing it means `capFrac` and
  the paint agreeing on ONE length, which predates this row.
- **The standing top is only checked when the ROW declares one.** `rescale_follow_series` reads
  `page["axes"]["domain"][1]` (`build_scene_timeline_f.py:3898`); a page born on the object's own derived scale
  (data range + 6 %) is not re-derived here, so "ymax must open the domain upward" is not checked for it. The
  player is safe either way - the solve clamps to 0 and nothing moves - but the refusal does not fire.
- **A follow tracks a MAXIMUM only.** A line that pushes a domain's FLOOR down (a fall out of the bottom) is the
  mirror case and is not built; the compiler refuses a target that does not open upward rather than half-working.
- **No effects card.** `docs/EFFECTS-CATALOG.jsonl` / `.md` are generated layers this lane must not rebuild
  (R26-92, the wall R26-219 / R26-221 / R26-223 / R26-226 all hit), so `follow` is not carded. Note also that
  `docs_find.py` reports eight layers STALE in this checkout with a failed `capabilities-index` build from before
  this lane - not mine to refresh.
- **Nothing under `content/video_engine/projects/` was touched.** The H unit's reveal row can now carry
