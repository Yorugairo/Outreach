## The bands, before and after — the numbers

The pair `page-life-still` / `page-life-live` at t = 9.0 (every series landed) and their `@proof-plus2`
halves at t = 11.0. **The pair cannot be read as one hash and never could be:** `live` = breath + drift,
so the live half's whole page is offset by its own exterior drift — the measured `.lp-page` matrix is
`translate(-1.879, -1.137)` at t = 9 against the still half's `translate(0, 0)`, with the breath term
identical on both (1.16104). The row's *"ZERO on the tick/title bands"* is therefore unreachable in
absolute pixels, and reading it that way would have meant deleting the exterior drift the operator keeps.
What IS the rule, exactly: **a word band is the still band moved rigidly by that ONE page-wide number and
nothing else; an electric band is not.** So each band is matched at every whole-pixel offset within ±8 and
two numbers are read — which offset fits best, and what share of the band still differs after it.

**Which offset each band fits (t = 9):** before, every word fitted its own — because every word walked at
its own phase, which is what read as chaos. After, there is one mechanism on the page and every band finds
it.

place that ANOTHER whole-pixel offset fitted well — which is the defect itself. The two columns only
separate when both are read: before, seven bands, seven offsets; after, one offset, and it is the page's
own. The electric is the control: 41.7 - 79.2 % of those bands differ at the best offset in both columns,

## The reveal — a line's pointer is ONE thing (clause 4)

Read off the DOM rather than the frame, because the claim is about what moves RELATIVE to what: every box
is taken against `.lp-page`'s own box, so the page's exterior breath and drift are divided out.

At t = 5.6 / 5.8 / 6.0, all four series DRAWING (`stroke-dashoffset` 30.3 / 47.4 / 72.1 / 137.2, then
15.7 / 24.5 / 37.3 / 70.9, then 8.1 / 12.6 / 19.3 / 36.6 — identical in both halves):

- **no element inside `.lp-page` carries a `style.translate` at all**, in either half. Before: 15 did (3
  divs + 12 chart texts, e.g. `DIV -2.19px 0.4px`, `text 4.16px -1.54px`).
- **0 of 15 words** sit in a different page-relative place in the live half than in the still half (to
  0.001 px): the title, the sub, the citation, the three y ticks, the axis caption, the four x ticks and
  the four end tags.
- the live half's four lead points carry the halo (`drop-shadow(rgba(...) 0.22) ... px`) at a radius of
  6.45 - 9.04 stage px on the spark's clock; the still half's are the plain `r = 6` tip with no filter.
- between 5.8 and 6.0 the lead points travel **7.36 / 11.61 / 17.64 / 34.72 px** while no word travels
  more than **1.97 px** — and that 1.97 px is the page's own breath (1.16162 -> 1.16325), a radial slide
  that grows with a word's distance from the page centre: the title 0.36, the sub 0.51, the citation 1.53.
  One pointer, and the words carried by the one breath.
