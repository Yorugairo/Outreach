# T2D binding addendum — thumb exterior envelope

Operator amendment, 2026-09-24. This adds to the frozen `WORK-ORDER-HAND-ORIGINAL-SURFACE-T2D.md`; it supersedes that order's wider “index/middle” thumb-placement wording.

- Valid thumb pose: along the radial **side** of the closed fist through, at most, resting over the **outside/top of the index finger**. The thumb stays external to the finger stack in all views.
- Invalid: thumb across middle/ring fingers, under curled fingers, entering the palm, or intersecting a finger/palm surface. Nearest-distance or a single camera silhouette is insufficient proof; inspect depth order and evaluated surfaces from dorsal, palmar, side and 540×960 views.
- First static art checkpoint and any staged rig proof must satisfy this envelope. Do not approve an anatomically wrong fist merely because its knuckle silhouette is compact.
