# Rig math review — bounded findings

Sources: `docs/content-video-engine/48-THE-FIGURE-AND-THE-GROUND.md` §§48.1–48.7; `complete_research_evidence_bundle/09_2d_and_2_5d_body_animation_object_handling_and_grounding.md` §§2.3–2.5, 3.2, 4.1, Blueprint G; `RIG-ACCEPTANCE.md`.

- **2-bone IK:** clamp `d` to the annulus, but return/report the clamped end and residual; Blueprint G’s `endJoint: target` can falsely claim an unreachable target. Its returned forearm-angle formula is also wrong: `l1=l2=1`, root `(0,0)`, target `(1,0)`, hinge `+1` yields shoulder `60°` and forearm `120°`, missing the target. Use the interior-angle form `θ₂=θ₁−s(π−β)` and verify by FK. Guard `d≈0`, `|l1-l2|`, fully extended, zero-length bones, and joint-limit infeasibility. Cache a pole/bend sign to prevent elbow flips.
- **FK/IK handoff:** seed FK angles and velocities from IK at release. Blend over the stated 100–150 ms, but unwrap 2D angles and sign-align unit (dual) quaternions/complex rotations before interpolation; antipodal or near-zero blended sums need a deterministic fallback.
- **DQS:** `encode`/`apply` and q/−q alignment pass the six tests, but this is a planar z-axis specialization, not a general 3D point skinner. Normalized rigid transforms do not prove whole-mesh area preservation. Require nonnegative weights summing to one, validate transform keys before indexing, then test triangle signed-area/edge distortion and degenerate joint neighborhoods; flag near-zero blend norms. A dot≈0 hemisphere choice is deterministic, not semantically unique.
- **Contact/ground:** measure rendered surface/collider signed distance and penetration, not joints alone. During plant, report max `v_contact−v_surface`/integrated slip against the homography; add hysteresis at plant/release and body-radius clearance for falls.
- **Punch timing:** rest-to-rest minimum jerk ends with zero velocity. Do not place impact at that endpoint; split approach/contact/recoil so glove surface contact precedes head recoil and follow-through remains visible.
