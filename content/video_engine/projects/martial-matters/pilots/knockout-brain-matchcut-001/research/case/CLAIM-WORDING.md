## Recommended news-peg copy

### Narration-safe version

> “The story resurfaced at UFC 331 after Sean Sharaf publicly accused Gable
> Steveson during the press conference. The verified legal record is narrower:
> Hennepin County prosecutors investigated the 2019 allegations, then filed no
> charges, citing conflicting accounts and inadequate evidence to fairly
> prosecute.”

This keeps the current event as news while separating a fighter’s accusation
from the official case status. Replace “publicly accused” with the exact
attribution only after the recovered press-conference footage is checked.

### Short caption / source card

> “2019 criminal-sexual-conduct investigation — no charges filed.”  
> “Hennepin County Attorney’s Office, Dec. 20, 2019.”  
> “No-charge decision is not a trial verdict.”

### If the direct quote is necessary

Use the form:

> “At the UFC 331 press conference, Sean Sharaf called Steveson a ‘rapist’ —
> an accusation, not a court finding. In 2019, Hennepin County prosecutors
> filed no charges after reviewing the investigation.”

The quote must remain visibly attributed to Sharaf/the recovered clip. The UFC
page archived in `sources/ufc-331-pre-fight-press-conference.html` identifies
the event but does not provide a transcript, so the quote is not independently
certified by that page.

## Editorial guardrails

- Keep “alleged,” “investigation,” “no charges filed,” and the prosecutor’s
  attributed explanation in separate clauses or cards.
- Do not show the brain animation as a metaphor for guilt, deception, or a
  “loophole.” It may explain the knockout mechanics only.
- Do not identify the complainant or repeat unverified details of the alleged
  conduct. The Hennepin County Attorney expressly said the office would not
  release details because it did not want to re-victimize the victim.
- Do not imply that the 2019 allegation caused the knockout, or that the
  knockout proves or disproves the allegation.
- Keep this package and any recovered press-conference/fight footage
  `review_only` / `render_eligible=false` until the operator reviews the
  source, wording, rights, and cut.

## Confidence and limits

- **High confidence:** dates, the June interim status, the December no-charge
  decision, the prosecutor’s quoted rationale, and the distinction between no
  charges and a trial verdict; all come from the official Hennepin releases.
- **Medium confidence pending direct-video check:** the exact wording and
  speaker attribution in the UFC 331 press-conference clip. The official UFC
  page establishes the event but carries no transcript.
- **Not established:** whether the alleged conduct occurred, the complainant’s
  account, any factual “innocence” or “guilt,” a civil/administrative outcome,
  or any court disposition beyond the official statement that no charges were
  filed.
