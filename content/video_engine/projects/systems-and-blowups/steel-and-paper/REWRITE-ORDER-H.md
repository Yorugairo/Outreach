## Editorial changes (the reason for the rewrite)

1. **The credit-first opening has been tested; it is retired as the OPENING.**
   G's order chose shape (b), "credit Bravos by name and add the layer it
   needed." The result is in: it converted the one viewer who searched
   *"bravos research"* (12:56 of 13:27, 96.2 % viewed) and lost the AI cohort
   (*"ai bubble"*: 5 views, 1:15, 9.4 % viewed). The first 90 seconds were,
   in effect, written for the person who already knows Bravos.
   **The credit is kept — it is honest and it is ours to give — but it moves
   behind our own claim.** It is a beat, not an entrance.

2. **Our claim and our number come first.** The strongest figure in the
   opening is the layer Bravos never drew — memory, +613 % — and it currently
   sits at 1:00 underneath someone else's chart. It belongs in front of the
   Bravos setup.

3. **The promise lands inside G09.** Target ≤ 0:45; 0:60 is a hard fail.

4. **The thumbnail is answered by sentence 1 (J12/G45).** Title says AI; the
   first "AI" in the shipped cut is at 0:17.6 and is a description of what
   *other people* say. The viewer clicked for AI and got 17 seconds of 1845.

5. **The spike and the railroad plates are NOT the problem and are not cut.**
   The suggested-traffic sources that feed this video are future- and
   consequence-framed (*"What Fills the Empty Space After Oil Is Gone?"*,
   *"it's boring, but it's what bursts the ai bubble"*, *"The AI Coding Boom
   Is Backfiring"*) — with one instructive exception, a *Margin Call*
   fire-sale clip. That is history deployed as **the moment the mechanism
   fires**, not history as preamble. Same distinction here: the spike is the
   image for the mechanism, used when the mechanism is being shown. It is not
   the entrance.

6. **Front-end density from the shorts lane.** Hook at 0:03, mechanism by
   0:10, our claim before anyone's context. E21 measured G's opening minute as
   the *thinnest* of the video — exactly inverted.
   **Import the density and the ordering, not the shorts SHAPE.** The
   one-mechanism / N-instances / ring structure would flatten the body that is
   currently holding for nine minutes. The body is the asset.

7. **Drop the weekly-disclosure cadence (operator, 2026-09-08).**
   *"They're the steel — and I own them on purpose, positions disclosed every
   week."* → keep the ownership disclosure, drop *"positions disclosed every
   week"*. The disclosure is the ethics; the cadence was a promise to sustain.

8. **Keep the tripwire commitment (operator, 2026-09-08).**
   *"Subscribe for the watch: the week either tripwire moves — Bravos' at five
   and a half, mine on memory — that video posts."* Operator will honour it.

9. **De-perish the time anchors.** All live figures were re-verified by the
   operator (2026-09-08) and **hold** — there is no factual repair in this
