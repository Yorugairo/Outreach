## J12 — the first sentence answers what the thumbnail poses — **FAIL**

The thumbnail poses **"STEEL or PAPER?"** over two measured bars: *History's
biggest bet, 1845* at $1T+ in today's money, against *Every AI Dollar, 2026*
at $690B. It asks the viewer to sort today's money into one of two bins, and
it has already put 1845 on the board as the comparison.

Sentence 1 is *"The safest thing you own looks like this."*

It answers nothing the thumbnail asked. It poses a **second** riddle — what is
the safest thing you own? — and defers the answer to sentence 2, and the
deictic "this" points at an image the viewer has not been given yet. A viewer
who clicked on a sorting question is made to hold a new question instead. The
words steel, paper, AI and 1845 are all absent from the first two sentences.

This is the mechanical G45 failure confirmed by reading. The fix is available
in the script's own material: the three questions **are** the sort the
thumbnail promises, and the spike **is** the steel. The opening should do the
sorting in sentence one, not hold a riddle in front of it.
