Four faults, one stretch:
1. The first proof that is OURS (28 cents) is hedged in the very next
   sentence, and the hedge is meta ("for this channel") - production
   scaffolding leaking into the argument.
2. 30s of restating, agreeing with, and approvingly quoting the
   counterparty, with no claim of ours. The viewer was promised the
   answer; for 37s the video IS the thing it promised to answer.
3. The evidence layer is dark 3:10-3:46 (36s, three bare plates) - the
   plate-cadence rule is honored nowhere in it, and it lands on the
   softest script.
4. The re-hook (biography twist + "they fail by pointing") is correct
   and works - it just arrives ~30s after the tension drained.

What changed, and why:
- CUT "The yardstick is a new instrument for this channel - no threshold
  on it yet. We're reading it together, one quarter at a time." (6s).
  The proof ends on its heaviest word; nothing hedges it.
- CUT "And here's the most honest sentence a macro channel has said all
  year: 'Don't try to call the top.'" (5s). Five seconds of praising
  the opponent with no turn. (If the quote is wanted, it may stay ONLY
  if the turn follows in the same breath: "'Don't try to call the top.'
  Agreed. And that is exactly where the chart goes wrong.")
- KEPT the three-beat concession ("Put my agreement on the record. The
  cycle is real. The threshold is real.") - conceding is the
  de-tribalized steelman and the head-fake setup - but it is now the
  LAST thing before the turn, not the middle of a 30s run.
- ADDED one bridge, "And that is exactly where the chart goes wrong,"
  so the concession is felt as a setup, not a surrender, and the
  biography twist lands as the answer to it.
- The turn moves from 3:23 to ~3:10, ahead of the measured drop.

## The gate this adds (STRENGTH-LOOP U6 / ruling E20)

A run of agreement with the counterparty - steelman, concession,
approving quote - never exceeds ~10s / two sentences without a claim of
OURS landing. A delivered instrument or proof is never hedged or
qualified in the sentence that follows it. Screen: mark every sentence
that restates or agrees with the counterparty; any run longer than two
without our own claim fails; any proof followed by a qualifier fails.
