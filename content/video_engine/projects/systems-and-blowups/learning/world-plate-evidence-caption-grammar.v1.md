# World Plate, Evidence, and Caption Grammar v1

Status: accepted design direction  
Accepted: 2026-08-15  
Supersedes: the caption-placement portion of `world-plate-evidence-rail-breakthrough.v1.md`

## Purpose

World plates provide the story environment. Literal deck/PDF crops provide the
claim surface. Canonical word-timed captions guide the viewer to the active
claim without becoming a competing screen layer.

## Production rules

1. Keep the selected world plate full-frame and legible as the hero.
2. Draw source evidence into semantic negative space. A plate may receive up
   to three artifacts sequentially. Completed cards may remain together when
   they form one reading set: keep no more than two in a horizontal row and
   place a third in a distinct vertical slot. The hand still draws only one
   card at a time.
3. A source card may use a non-destructive, source-bound crop to enlarge its
   legible whiteboard region. Crop to the outside edge of the teacher stamp;
   never clip, replace, or separately recreate that stamp. The source file,
   factual approval, and provenance remain unchanged.
4. Keep canonical word-timed captions in one transparent, standard lower-third
   anchor across the sequence. Evidence may move by semantic slot, but captions
   must not follow it around the frame. Use a subtle text shadow; do not use an
   opaque panel or a clipped fixed-height box.
5. Preserve canonical caption words and timings. Layout changes may never
   rewrite, split independently, or crop those words.
6. The hand exists only while an evidence artifact is being revealed. A card
   should remain for roughly six seconds when its parent plate has room; a
   subsequent hand reveal may begin after the first has finished drawing.
7. A slow Ken-Burns reframe may breathe inside a world plate or image-led
   crop after drawing completes. It is not a substitute for a scene change and
   must not compromise source-card typography.
8. Select scene transitions by semantics, with a deliberately small palette:
   clean cut for contrast or correction; a brief directional wipe for a process
   continuation; and a sparing 3D book-flip for a structural reframe. Keep the
   evidence layer still and absent from the decorated handoff.
9. Do not promote a full-scene hand-draw replacement from the evidence-card
   reveal pattern. It requires isolated scene artwork, calibrated hand/nib
   choreography, coverage QA, and a separate operator gate.
