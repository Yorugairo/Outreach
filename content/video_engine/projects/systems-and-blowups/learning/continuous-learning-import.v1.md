## Action

Use a small semantic transition palette: clean cut for a sharp correction or
contrast, brief directional wipe for a process continuation, and a sparing 3D
book-flip for a structural reframe. Give each world plate a restrained Ken
Burns direction, but do not turn every motion change into an effect. Schedule
decorated transitions on evidence-free boundaries; keep literal source cards
still while they are being read and preserve canonical word timings.

## Exclusion

Full-scene hand-draw replacement is not production-ready. A successful
hand-drawn evidence-card reveal does not validate it: it needs isolated scene
art, a calibrated hand/nib path, coverage QA, and an operator-approved proof.

## Evidence

`current-bubble-mechanism/fresh-60s-transition-evidence-v4` was accepted with
Ken Burns, native book-flips, directional wipe, and clean cuts. The hand-drawn
full-scene transition was explicitly removed.
