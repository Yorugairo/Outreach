# World Plate + Evidence Rail Breakthrough v1

Status: accepted design direction
Proven by: `current-bubble-mechanism/finance-world-plate-evidence-rail-proof-v1`
Accepted: 2026-08-10

## Durable composition contract

Use the canonical woodblock world plate as the full-frame story environment.
Treat deck/PDF crops as literal evidence artifacts that enter through one
reserved callout or inset at a time. The world carries continuity and mood;
the evidence crop carries the sourced claim. Do not turn the frame into a
collage of competing cards.

## Atomic rules

1. **World owns attention.** Keep the selected world plate full-frame for the
   beat; preserve its characters, spatial logic, and negative space.
2. **Evidence gets one slot.** Reveal at most one source crop per active beat,
   using a reserved lower rail or inset zone matched to the plate.
3. **Reveal the artifact, not a replacement screen.** Use a hand-led mask or
   trace reveal, retain the crop’s baked source typography, and write only the
   small local label/caption needed for orientation.
4. **Separate provenance from decoration.** Source crops remain literal and
   source-bound; generated world art remains context, not evidence.
5. **Exit cleanly.** Remove or replace the evidence crop before introducing a
   new one, unless the narration explicitly requires a comparison.
