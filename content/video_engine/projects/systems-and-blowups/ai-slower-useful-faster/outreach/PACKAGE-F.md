Title: Slowing AI Could Speed Up the Economy

Description:

AI finishes the draft in seconds. You spend the afternoon fixing it.

What if the next economic breakthrough is getting that afternoon back? This short makes the case for testing today's AI on finished work—and the human supervision it still needs—not just impressive demos.

The twenty-step comparison is an illustration, not a product benchmark. The question is whether improving reliability could free more productive time than another push at the frontier.
