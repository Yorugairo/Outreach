### The Mathematical Macro Mechanism
- **Negative Effective Rate of Protection (ERP)**:
  $$\text{ERP} = \frac{t_f - \sum a_i t_i}{1 - \sum a_i} = \frac{0.15 - (0.60 \times 0.25)}{0.40} = 0.00\%$$
  When Section 232 steel and aluminum surcharges apply ($t_i \approx 32\%$):
  $$\text{ERP} = \frac{0.15 - (0.60 \times 0.32)}{0.40} = -10.5\%$$
  *Result:* The tariff structure taxes domestic auto manufacturing more heavily than foreign finished vehicle imports.
