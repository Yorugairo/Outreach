## 1. The 5 Core Mechanics of the "New Bravos Style"

To reproduce the institutional tier seen in *China Just Triggered A New World Order* without violating our brand doctrine:

1. **The Diegetic TV-Embed Stage (13% External Lane):**
   - External claims (cable news clips, financial pundits, Wall Street memos) play on a floating monitor mounted on a dimly lit, textured concrete studio wall.
   - Projected via planar homography ($\mathbf{H}$ with $1200\text{px}$ perspective, $\text{rotateY}(-4^\circ)$, $\text{rotateX}(2^\circ)$).
   - Micro-sine camera pan $A \sin(\omega u)$ introduces subtle parallax between the background wall ($Z = -100$), monitor bezel ($Z = -20$), video plane ($Z = 0$), and callout badges ($Z = +40$).
2. **The Stacking Press Card Dock:**
   - Real high-resolution article screenshots cropped tight to the headline and primary paragraph.
   - Key phrase underlined with an accent token stroke (`stroke-dasharray` reveal).
   - Cards stack along a diagonal depth offset with the newest card fully lit and older cards dimmed to 40% opacity.
3. **Kinetic Tip-Riding Callout Pills:**
   - On coordinate yield curves, pill badges (`US 10Y: 4.95%`, `US 30Y: 5.38%`) track the leading edge of the stroke path in real-time ($P_{\text{tip}}(u) = P_k + (uN - k)(P_{k+1} - P_k)$) with a dynamic horizontal leader line.
   - Badge pops into place with an analytic overshoot spring ($\text{springPop}, M_p = 0.05$).
4. **The "Scale-Break" Asymmetry Payoff:**
   - When revealing an extreme metric (e.g., $1.08T federal interest or the 2026 debt load), the chart's X-axis ceiling $X_{\max}(u)$ dynamically expands from $500\text{B}$ to $1,500\text{B}$ via an analytic spring, causing all baseline bars to compress to the left as the breakthrough bar shoots across the screen.
5. **Dual-Lane Viewport Split (52% / 48%):**
   - Left stage ($[0, 0.52W]$): Quantitative yield line coordinate engine.
   - Right stage ($[0.52W, W]$): Macro driver (geopolitical oil map with radial sonar ping, or corporate cash flow manifold).
