# Current Bubble — 90-second HyperFrames opening proof

This proof tells the viewer that the memory trade and the index-fund problem
must be judged by mechanism, not by the shared fact that both charts rose.

| Beat | Spoken turn | Object choreography | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Wrong bubble | Elevator world stays on screen; title tag settles into the supported car. | Establishes the thesis without cutting away from the mechanism. |
| 02 | Memory looks fake | Memory stack enters as a framed object and settles beside the elevator. | Makes the product, not the number, the new subject. |
| 03 | Three supports | Factory plate opens from the left while the memory frame remains visible. | Shows demand, capacity, and commitment as one braid. |
| 04 | Automatic inflows | The fund container expands from the factory braid and receives the next dollar. | Explains index-fund concentration as a flow, not a random chart. |
| 05 | Correction / bottleneck | Risk cards stack briefly, then the bottleneck plate opens at the gate. | Preserves the qualification while showing the physical constraint. |
| 06 | Symptom versus diagnosis | Inspection window holds while two deterministic panels bridge from price to mechanism. | Reframes the word “bubble” in place. |
| 07 | Belief versus support | Two panels separate; the right foundation grows under the support claim. | Makes the causal distinction visible. |
| 08 | Callback | Overlays retract and the original elevator world returns. | Pays off the opening metaphor instead of jumping to another image. |

Rhythm: settle → open → braid → expand → qualify → diagnose → resolve → callback.
