# Episode 3 Question Brief — How BJJ Became a Global Sport

This file is not an approved script or claim packet.

## Questions

- Which institutions, events, rules, and media systems drove expansion within
  Brazil?
- What evidence traces early and sustained transmission into the United States?
- How did modern competition rules reshape training, tactics, and public identity?
- Which migration, military, university, tournament, and media networks explain
  international growth without collapsing the story into one promoter?
- When and through which documented routes did organized BJJ reach the Pacific
  Northwest?
- What evidence can describe regional growth without endorsing a named academy?
- Which modern claims involve living-person likenesses or organization marks and
  therefore need explicit approval?

The episode ends with a generic Registry CTA. A named academy may appear as a
historical fact only when material, cited, neutrally framed, and rights-compliant.
