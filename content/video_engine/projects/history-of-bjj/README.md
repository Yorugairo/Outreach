# History of BJJ Series

This directory contains source-controlled editorial inputs for History Documentary
V4. Runtime jobs copy and hash these contracts; render outputs belong under the
gitignored runtime or `.context` evidence roots.

- Episode 1 is the acceptance pilot and may advance only to Research Gate without
  operator approval.
- Episodes 2 and 3 are question briefs. They are not factual scripts.
- Consultant and YouTube Reference Pack material is creative study only and never
  factual or render provenance.
- Research citations, visual-asset rights, and creative-study observations remain
  separate.

The master target is approximately 10 minutes (8–12 minutes). Two native Shorts
and chapter-level subvideos are compiled only from self-contained, approved claim
clusters.

Living-scene foundation artifacts:

- `communication-grammar.v1.json` owns the approved surfaces, motion, transition,
  comparison, and sound rules.
- `woodblock-style-packs.v1.json` defines the shared Combat Woodblock identity
  and its action, historical-editorial, and rapid whitespace variants.
- `series-asset-demand.json` is the finite three-episode demand map. It does not
  authorize generation or make any asset renderable.
- `world-packs.v1.json` defines six provider-neutral reusable environments.
- `series-scene-demand.json` defines the foundation scene families and explicit
  transition continuity. Episode 2–3 entries remain questions, not claims.

In this project a world pack is not a Google Flow scene. Flow Scenebuilder
sequences generated clips; world packs define the persistent visual ingredients
that may be supplied to Flow or another producer after asset approval.
