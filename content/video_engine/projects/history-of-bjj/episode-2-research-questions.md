# Episode 2 Question Brief — The Branches BJJ History Forgot

This file is not an approved script or claim packet.

## Questions

- What documentary evidence establishes George Gracie’s teaching, travel, and
  influence separately from later family narratives?
- Which Japanese-derived grappling communities developed in São Paulo outside the
  best-known Rio story?
- What is the documented history of the Lotus Club, and which claims come from
  institutional records versus retrospective promotion?
- How did immigration, regional identity, public contests, and organizational
  networks create parallel branches?
- Which people or communities are absent when BJJ history is reduced to a single
  family tree?
- Where do sources disagree, and what language accurately communicates that
  disagreement?
