```
Real-time market reactions: the viral chart, the big call, the Fed move, answered with the numbers.

When a chart goes viral, a major analyst makes a call, the Fed moves, or a chip earnings print lands, this playlist answers it - and shows the data underneath.

The format is a reply, not a recap. We take the source's case at full strength first: the exact chart, the exact claim, named. Then we run the data they didn't - filings, government statistics, contract prices, shipment and customs records - and we say whether the call holds. When they're right, we say so. When they're wrong, we show where the number breaks.

What gets a reply:
- A chart everyone is sharing - a "this is 1999 again" comparison, a bubble warning, two lines that used to move together and stopped
- A big call from a named analyst or desk - Bravos Research is where this playlist started
- A Fed rate decision and the bond-yield move around it
- Earnings from the companies that decide the AI trade: Nvidia, Micron, SK hynix, Samsung, TSMC, and the hyperscalers writing the checks
- A market crash headline, an index milestone, a "largest ever" anything

What stays the same in every reply: one named source, one measurable claim, one tripwire - the specific number that would flip the call - and a verdict you can check yourself before the next print.

Replies drop as the events happen, not on a schedule. The long-form explainers live in the main feed; this is the fast lane. Save the playlist and the newest answer is always on top.

Education, not investment advice.
```
