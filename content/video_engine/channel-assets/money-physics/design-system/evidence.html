Sharp, dark, real — the counterpoint to the print

  • Real charts built from real data; verified documents; cited source frames. Never info cards. Every chart prints its source line.
  • Each evidence moment names its document, exact figure, badge (numeral verbatim) and window. Evidence every 15–45 s; a bare stretch over 12 s needs a cause.
  • Preferred composite is diegetic: the host gestures at a surface and real evidence lands in it.
  • Scene shape (doc 29 Part 8): one Ken Burns move per scene → evidence build (dock + badges) → wipe → repeat. Docks snap in; badges reveal one at a time and never retract inside a scene; the wipe fires only after both docks clear.
  • Do not bury the plate. The world is never washed out under evidence.
