### Technique 2: The Trahan Fast Micro-Payoff (<15s Trust Loop)
- **Observed Timestamp & Shot ID:** Shot #22–#29 (`02:09 - 02:22`) — Ryan Trahan $5 TikTok microphone test.
- **Visual Effect:** Question raised at $t=0$ ("Will this sound good?"), physical action executed at $t=3$ (plugs it in), answer heard and confirmed at $t=7$ ("Question asked, question answered 5 seconds later").
- **Cognitive Function:** Solves the 34% drop in the first 30 seconds. Fast micro-payoffs prove the creator respects the viewer's time, shattering the expectation of watch-time stalling.
- **Application to Forensic Finance:** Deliver a concrete physical contrast inside 20 seconds (e.g. 1981 debt at 30% of GDP vs. today at 124% of GDP) before asking the viewer to commit to the macro 3-question audit roadmap.

### Technique 3: The "Second Mountain" Architecture (Back 1/3 Retention Anchor)
- **Observed Principle & Origin:** Zone 3 Midroll Sag & Outro Decay (`06:13 - 09:14`).
- **Diagnostic Origin:** Empirically verified against channel retention analytics (13:27 video holding flat at 38% through 8:30, then decaying to 20% by 13:00).
- **Mechanical Fix:**
