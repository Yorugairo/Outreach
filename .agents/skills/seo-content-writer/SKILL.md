# SEO Content Writer Guidelines

When writing marketing, SEO, or public-facing content for the National BJJ Registry and GoBJJ app, you MUST adhere to the following rules:

## 1. The Core Brand Identity: Pragmatic Value
We are not a charity, we are not benevolent saviors, and we are not zealots pushing an ideology. We are a **pragmatic, high-utility business tool**.
Our goal is to be the second largest expense at a gym (besides rent) strictly because we provide the most value.
Never sound preachy. Never complain about the "drama" or "negativity" in the BJJ community.

## 2. Top-of-Funnel vs. Bottom-of-Funnel
- **Top of Funnel (National BJJ Registry):** Our public registry surfaces siloed information (lineages, schedules, reviews) that was previously hidden in offline networks or "beneath the Google floor" (old forums). We turn the internet from a static yellow-pages phonebook into a dynamic, active lead generator for gyms.
- **Bottom of Funnel (GoBJJ):** Once the registry generates the lead, the GoBJJ app acts as the operational backend to handle the business growth, drive student retention, manage waivers, and streamline operations.

## 3. Tone and Flow Constraints
- **Do not use the term "certified black belt".** It is not a phrase commonly used in the community and sounds artificial. Use terms like "legitimate black belt", "authenticated lineage", or "world-class black belt".
- **Avoid cheesy, hard-sell transitions.** Never use phrases like *"That's why progressive academies choose BJJ Registry!"*. Let the utility speak for itself.
- **Show, Don't Tell.** When mentioning high-profile names or academies (e.g., Marcelo Alonso, James Foster), embed them naturally into a narrative of regional growth and historical context rather than just name-dropping for SEO.

## 4. Length and Depth
Articles must often exceed 2,000 words to establish high topical authority and accommodate dense entity mapping (name drops, gym names, locations). Ensure the flow remains natural and educational throughout.
