# Channel Positioning & Narrator Persona

## 1. The Three Channels

### 1. MONEY PHYSICS (*"The market isn't magic. It's mechanics."*)
- **Audience**: Investors, operators, market-watchers who want causal balance-sheet explanations.
- **Formats**: 
  - *Answer Format*: Agreeing-but-relocating replies to competitor claims (Doc 35).
  - *Tripwire Watch*: Falsifiable market inflection calls with concrete threshold levels.
  - *Mechanism Explainers*: Causal breakdown of financial and currency plumbing.
- **Core Dials**: **Risk. Reward. Time.**

### 2. BUILDING MONEY (*"Build your world."*)
- **Audience**: Builders, young operators, individuals compounding personal wealth.
- **Ground**: Personal & business finance viewed through what the operator actually builds (AI tooling, trade websites, channel economics, tracked costs).
- **Identity Rule (Ruling A2)**: Trades and business models are subjects, never personal identity.
- **Thesis**: AI compresses high-paid software work while making physical trade/information systems asymmetric bets. The camera always stays on the money.

### 3. MARTIAL MATTERS
- **Audience**: Combat athletes, martial artists, combat sports historians.
- **Series**: BJJ History (lineage documentaries), Marshall Matters, Martial Mental.
- **Canon**: Combat Woodblock aesthetic. Precise, authentic nomenclature.

## 2. Narrator Persona & The Biography Rule

- **Material, Never Trophy**: 
  Homeless teen who climbed out -> Dispensary owner (cash flow operator) -> JPMorgan bank risk analyst (institutional balance sheets) -> Growth strategy consultant today (scaling businesses & AI architectures) -> Trains BJJ.
- **The Twist Rule (Ruling A1)**: 
  Credentials arrive *after* the paradox as a subordinate clause twist (e.g. *"Running a dispensary before I landed in risk at JPMorgan — and today I consult on growth strategy — the first thing you learn is..."*). The resumé never gets its own sentence.
- **Systems Over Charts (Ruling A3)**: 
  The narrator's edge is risk architecture and institutional systems, not technical chart-drawing.
- **Conditional Contrarian**: 
  Dissent only when the consensus misprices strength as weakness and the risk is asymmetric. Always credit the opponent's thesis before relocating it.
