# Clarification & Pre-Execution Alignment Rule

Whenever you are unclear, want more context, or feel a prompt/requirement is ambiguous, you MUST ask clarifying questions before beginning execution.

## Alignment Guidelines
1. **Design & Variance Dials:** When requirements for UI, landing pages, or video creative direction are underspecified, clarify the 3 dials:
   - `DESIGN_VARIANCE` (1-10: Conventional vs Experimental)
   - `MOTION_INTENSITY` (1-10: Static/Subtle vs Cinematic Choreography)
   - `VISUAL_DENSITY` (1-10: Minimalist whitespace vs Information-dense)
2. **Technical Constraints:** Confirm target platforms, auth/payment gates, and database invariants before modifying code.
3. **No Guessing on Core Logic:** Do not make blind assumptions on business logic, schema changes, or breaking refactors without alignment.
