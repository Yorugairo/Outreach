## Licensing rules (hard gates)

1. **CC0 / public domain**: free for monetized use, no credit needed.
2. **CC-BY**: allowed, but artist + track + license link MUST land in
   the video description — see SOP below.
3. **BANNED: CC-NC** (non-commercial — we are monetized) and **CC-ND**
   (no-derivatives technically forbids syncing audio against a video
   timeline at all).
4. **BBC Sound Effects archive: banned** for our use — free tier is
   non-commercial only.
5. AI-generated music can still trip similarity-based Content ID
   claims: keep the Suno generation record (prompt, date, account) per
   track as dispute evidence.

## Attribution SOP

Every sound asset carries its row in the project's `sound/SOURCES.md`
(licence, source, provenance). An asset missing its ledger entry does not
ship (same rule as an unsourced chart).

**The package does not carry CC-BY credit today.** The description is
generated by `publish_package.py` (PIPELINE stage 8, R26-8 / P52 T16: "No
hand text lives in the tool"), which has no attribution field, and no
`ATTRIBUTIONS.md` exists. No shipped asset so far is CC-BY: the cues are CC0
(Freesound), the beds Suno Pro. Licensing rule 2 still binds: before a CC-BY asset ships, its line ("Track" by Artist, licensed
CC-BY 4.0, link) must reach the description by hand (a hand-written
description outranks the generated one - PIPELINE stage 8); the record has no
automated path for it yet.

## Design doctrine pointer

Cues are authored in the project's `sound/SOUND-PLAN.json` (`page_cues`,
page-relative - doc 41 §6) and placed by `build_short.py`'s cue map
(CAPABILITIES "Page MOUNT entry + the short's sound map"). They score events
the build already timestamps - a page's roll-out, a return, the suck, a card
throw; the sound pass does not invent its own timeline. Three rules bound them:

- **A mount has no sound cue** - the page turn is the thing the mount removes
  (doc 29 §9.31).
- **M29:** no transient cue (a bed is exempt) lands inside 0:05-0:12 unless a
  page lands with it within 1.5s; the transient gain is 0.08 (CAPABILITIES,
  2026-09-12).
- **The cue matches the motion class, and a whoosh leads.** Movement gets a
  whoosh, a collision an impact; a whoosh starts about 0.3 s early so its
  energy peak lands on the motion frame instead of trailing it (727.55 ->
  727.25 s on the burst, 2026-09-01, LEDGER `650f7eac755e`, `359159007f80`).
  A landing sound sits on the contact frame - 1 (CAPABILITIES "Stop-action
  mechanics").

## Levels

- **The short's bed: −20 LU, and no louder** (E54, 2026-09-08; the bed half
  stands after E70). The earlier platform split in CAPABILITIES "Beds and the
  press pack" (YouTube −28 LU / Facebook −26, 2026-09-05) predates it; the
  gain formula there, `bed_gain = 10^((VO_I − LU − bed_I)/20)`, still applies.
- **Accents are background sounds, 8-10 dB under the voice** (Tokyo
  `sound/SOURCES.md`, 2026-09-05) - the starting reference for every video, tuned by ear from there (E81).
- A long-form bed level is not re-ruled after E54 in the record opened for
  this audit; read `docs/research/audio/SUBTHRESHOLD_BACKGROUND_MUSIC_RESEARCH_BLUEPRINT.md`
  before setting one.
