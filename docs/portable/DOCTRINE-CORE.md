# DOCTRINE CORE — the NotebookLM export

Audience: NotebookLM (a 10,000-character source limit), pasted whole. It is
the condensed doctrine, not the rulebook. Agents working IN this repo do not
load it: they read the full doc set through the index (`docs_find.py`,
`OPERATOR-RULINGS.md`, the canonical docs), which wins on any conflict.
Audited against the record 2026-09-13 (P54).

**THE ORDER OF WHAT MATTERS (E27).** First: the package answered - thumbnail,
title, hook - deeply, in the first sentence. Second: the voice is constant.
Everything else keeps a viewer the package earned; none of it earns one. A
still image with a voice that answered its title beat a channel that
out-produced it.

## OPERATION

Faceless YouTube production, three channels, one animated host voice, never
a live face. **World view, voice and logic standards are INVARIANT** - that
constant is the brand. Per lane only **delivery style** and **evidence
precision** calibrate.

1. **MONEY PHYSICS** - investors. Formats: answer format (agreeing-but-
   relocating replies to big finance channels), tripwire watch (falsifiable
   calls), mechanism explainers. "The market isn't magic. It's mechanics."
   Dials: Risk. Reward. Time.
2. **BUILDING MONEY** - youth building a path. Personal and business
   finance shown through what the operator actually does: AI builds, trades
   websites, channel economics, tracked real costs. Trades and info systems
   are SUBJECTS, never the identity. Thesis: AI compresses high-paid
   software work and turns info-systems work into near trade-work - both
   are asymmetric bets. Camera stays on the money. "Build your world."
3. **MARTIAL MATTERS** - martial artists and fight fans. Series: BJJ
   History, Marshall Matters, Martial Mental. Name techniques properly,
   never fake depth. World set (Combat Woodblock canon).

## NARRATOR

Biography is material, never trophy: homeless teen who climbed out; bank
risk analyst (a decade trading his own money); dispensary owner; builds
with AI; trains jiu jitsu. Rooms are portfolios, markets are machines.
"More tech than the finance guys, more finance than the tech guys." Edge
is risk analysis and systems thinking, not charts or code. Conditional
contrarian: dissents only when the market prices strength as weakness and
the risk is asymmetric. Never calls a famous investor dumb - credits the
frame that made them right, then explains the shift. Voice: direct,
compressed, concrete; numbers and mechanisms beat adjectives; humor
playful in setup, cutting on landing; never tribal; never doom without a
dial. Every video ends empowering - an action the viewer runs tonight.
"The loss is proof of participation, not failure."

## RETENTION CLOCK (judged by ear, never on the page)

0-3s the hook, first sentence, answering the thumbnail; no atmosphere,
greeting or history. By 0:10 answer it enough to prove the video keeps
promises. By 0:45 an explicit, named promise of payout. Then the cycle
repeats every beat - hook, prove, promise, deliver - forever. Time beats
against the spoken take, never word counts.

## LONG FORM (six phases)

**Long form is never under 8 minutes (E74).** Open and close are 60-90s
at any runtime; midpoint pinned 45-55%; middle units are the runtime knob
- systems MERGE, never drop.

**P1 OPEN** - 0-3s microhook, surprising word LAST, screen already moving.
The hook's chart opens on its axes and is answered on the ledger at once
(E44/E73) - never a full unannotated chart. By 0:08 pay the hook WRONG
(paradox). 0:08-0:30 story: archetypes, "you" enters, stakes by 0:25; biography
lands after the paradox, as a twist. By 0:45 real partial value FIRST,
then a DATED, calculable promise (rehook #1; the licensed triad). To 1:30
map-not-territory signpost; opponent always a MECHANISM, never a villain;
rehook #2; every abstraction cashed into an object or number.

**P2 ENGINE** (to ~17%) - catalyst as a closed micro loop; new info every
15-30s; beats join by BUT/THEREFORE, never AND-THEN. Plant the HEAD-FAKE
straight, no wink; demolition waits for the pivot. Rehook + second
promise-tease near 10%. Macro loop 1 closes on a partial answer opening a
bigger question. Only mid-video CTA slot: 15-30s after this payoff
(default skip).

**P3 GAP** (to 45%) - repeat the unit: anecdote run → BUT gap → THEREFORE
deeper move → reflection (anaphora, evolved tail; register SHIFTS each
unit) → rehook. Best evidence banked for the last pre-pivot unit. Final
promise-tease ~27%; after it, zero re-promises.

**P4 PIVOT** (45-55%) - signpost, pause, the reversal in contracted
sentences; the head-fake resolves into it; the central object flips
meaning. Stakes in numbers. Zero CTAs.

**P5 REFLECTION** (to ~87%) - thesis at the chiastic center. GRAND PAYOFF
at 60-70%, never "as promised"; the promised calculation at viewer scale.
Loops close completely, reverse order. THE TELL: variable + threshold +
current position + flip condition. Ring stays open; ≥2 savor beats.

**P6 CLOSE** (final 60-90s) - ring echo: the opening image returns
transformed, never narrating the symmetry. FINAL TRIAD fused with the
anaphora's last form; terminal stress. ONE CTA 15-30s after the payoff:
value named → subscribe FOR the named next thing → action→outcome.
Story Close (default) or Cliffhanger Bridge (serialized = zero CTA). Hard
stop.

**Roster** (30-min ref; scale by count, never cut pivot/promise/ring):
rehooks 4 +1/unit; 3 promise sightings → 1 payoff; macro loops 5-8 LIFO;
micro loop per 30-60s; 2-4 callbacks, ring last; ≥1 head-fake; ≤1 mid +
1 close CTA; ≥2 savor; 1 tell; 1 ring.

## SHORTS (a different, cheaper machine - E35, E41, E44)

45-60s, exactly one mechanism. 0-3s hook; the chart rolls out under
sentence two - the chart is the mechanism, the figure the stakes; host
after the page. Then the same mechanism N times (one spine, never a pile),
a rehook every 30s, the ring back to the mechanism. Conjoined beats
compress, never clip words. No sentence over 18 words (the caption strip).
The script never writes the brand triad - the outro carries it.

## DEVICES

**Dual engine is master**: anecdote (action raising questions) alternating
with reflection (meaning); the ratio shifts by phase (open ~80/20 → pivot
50/50 → reflection 30/70 → close 40/60, ending on image) but alternation
NEVER stops - flag any single-engine run over 30s. **Triads**: max 3 per long
form (promise, optional engine, final). **Anaphora**: constant opening,
EVOLVING tail, resolved in the final triad. **Terminal stress** at every
scale. **Counterpoint**: narration never captions its visual. **Pauses**:
the script marks only `[pre-key]` before reveals and `[post-key]` after
settles, ≈3/min (G18); nothing else in brackets. The recorder strips them
(37 §21) and the editor cuts the beats in; no tag reaches the voice.
Deadly: delay disease, context dumping, mid-video cliff, grand-payoff
betrayal.

## SENTENCE GATES (every sentence passes all ten)

1 one idea · 2 active, named subject, never an agent-hiding passive ·
3 terminal stress · 4 abstractions cashed in the sentence · 5 length 5-22
words (18 max on a short; fragments only in licensed pivot/triad runs or
chart-reads) · 6 ear test - no homophones, page-isms, circumlocutions; proper nouns over
descriptions · 7 attribution BEFORE assertion · 8 delete test · 9 strong
verbs · 10 nothing from NEVER. Master check: which node, which roster
duty, which scale does the device repeat at? None = cut. The strength pass
is STANDING (won a seed-locked A/B by ear): rewrite any failing sentence
until it passes.

## ANSWER FORMAT (Money Physics reactions)

We never compete on the source's chart-reading; their evidence is cited
generously, with their branding. **Their evidence, our relocation.**
CREDIT → STEELMAN (their numbers) → AGREE on record → LOCATE the claim →
RELOCATE with THEIR attributed numbers → MECHANISM → CONSEQUENCE (viewer-
scale calculation) → ESCALATE both ways, then their tell beside ours
(stricter).

## TITLES + THUMBNAILS = ONE CLICK-CONTRACT

Title: tension in the first ~40 chars, ≤~55 total; specificity beats
cleverness; every promise paid. Patterns: relocation claim, falsifiable
stake, paradox, cost receipt. Thumbnail: ONE subject, extreme contrast,
reads at 168px; ≤3 punch words, only if the image is ambiguous; title and
thumb split the work, never repeat; motifs compound across the catalogue;
no real faces. Thumbnails sell the question or thesis, never the opening prop.

## PRODUCTION

**The chart is the world (E61).** Long form is authored on the ledger
page: real charts from real data, verified documents, cited source frames.
A chart proves the sentence being spoken and leaves when it has. A plate
(stylized art, host in-world) is used for one named reason: a landing
surface for docked evidence, a bridge between ideas, or a reset that
clears the world. Preferred on a plate: the DIEGETIC COMPOSITE - the host
gestures at a surface, real evidence lands in it. Never info cards. Nothing
is ever still: no 12s without a visual event (8s target); evidence at least
every 45s, P1 and P6 included (E21). Each evidence moment names its
document, exact figure and window. Captions word-timed, kinetic.

Narration: numbers as spoken words, exact numerals on screen. Voice by
platform (E70): Facebook ships Chirp; YouTube's voice is OPEN, and no
YouTube cut ships on a synthetic body voice until it is settled.
Unverified claims NEVER go inline; write narration to survive their
removal. No advice framing: mechanisms, never prescriptions.

## NEVER

Greetings · "in this video" · "let that sink in" · stock filler · bait
questions ("…right?") · fake curiosity · any CTA outside the two windows ·
passives hiding actors · uncashed abstractions · announcing structure ·
fabricated figures · bracketed notes in narration · a claim you are unsure
is verified (it isn't).
