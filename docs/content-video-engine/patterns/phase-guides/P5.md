# P5 — REFLECTION & CONVERGENCE

> Self-contained generation guide. Consume ONLY: the INJECTION block, the
> P4 ledger, and this file. Every line answers: which structural node,
> which retention duty, what scale. Lines that answer none get cut.

## Function

The meaning engine takes over. The transformative thesis is stated at the
video's chiastic center, the promise made in the open is PAID as a natural
culmination, every loop opened since 0:00 closes completely, and the
narrator puts competence on record with one falsifiable claim. Validation
is this phase's emotional product: the viewer's invested attention pays
off — an unclosed loop here converts investment into resentment.

New-information cadence relaxes here to every 30–45 seconds — never to
zero. Reflection without fresh material is a sermon.

## Beat template

### The chiastic center (phase start)

- THE transformative thesis — the one sentence the video exists to earn —
  framed `[pre-key]` + line + `[post-key]`. Sentence rhythm EXPANDS back
  to full cadenced 12–15 word waves (the pivot's contraction releases).
- Visuals go slow and large: macro imagery, abstract synthesis — the
  screen thinks while the voice concludes.

### The grand payoff (60–70% of runtime) — MANDATORY position

- The promise from the open — teased at ~10% and ~27%, never since — is
  **paid in full as a natural culmination**, not announced ("as I
  promised" is banned). If the promise was calculable, the calculation
  happens NOW, on screen, at viewer scale ("your rent, divided by your
  square feet, times the footprint of the thing").
- **Every opened loop closes COMPLETELY, in reverse order of opening** —
  last opened, first closed. Partial closes and "more on that another
  time" are failures. List each close.

### The falsifiable tell (post-payoff)

- One claim with all four parts: **the variable** being watched, **the
  threshold** that matters, **the current position**, and **the flip
  condition** ("if X crosses Y, I'm wrong, and here's what I'd do").
  Competence goes on record; this is what makes the narrator worth
  subscribing to — affection is built on checkable honesty, not charisma.
- The empowerment turn starts here: hand the viewer the instrument
  ("run your own number tonight").

### The callback cascade (phase end)

- Remaining planted tokens close **newest-first** — every object,
  phrase, or person planted earlier is re-sighted with its earned
  meaning. The RING token does NOT close here; it is reserved for the
  final echo.
- **≥2 savor beats**: lines that have earned silence get it — `[post-key]`
  after each; any silence longer than ~3 seconds belongs to the visual
  timeline, not the narration.
- One breathing dip mid-phase (plus one per gap unit if a unit ran).

## MANDATORY: Glass alternation — phase ratio ≈ 30/70 reflection

Reflection dominates but the anecdote engine NEVER stops: every 2–3
reflection sentences, drop back into a concrete image, a person, a moment
("the rowing machine", "the drawer", "March"). Reflection that loses its
anecdotal ground floats away; this ratio is the guard against the lecture
drift that kills finance content.

## MANDATORY: tricolon/anaphora placement

- The anaphora phrase makes its LAST evolved recurrence here and is left
  positioned for its final form (do not deliver the final form — the
  close owns it).
- One tricolon is permitted ONLY if the thesis line itself is triadic;
  otherwise zero. A phonetic anchor (alliteration/assonance) is licensed
  on the payoff line and the tell — nowhere else.

## HARD GATE: attribution-first

Source before assertion, every claim. The payoff's arithmetic must trace
to attributed figures already established (or introduced here WITH their
source). The tell's current-position claim must be attributable or
first-person verifiable. `[verify]` may not touch the payoff or the tell.

## Counterpoint: abstract synthesis

The screen abstracts (maps, aggregates, motion of numbers) while the
voice stays human and specific — or the inverse. The one forbidden state
is both channels abstract at once: that is the lecture, visualized.

## Pause marks

`[pre-key]` + `[post-key]` framing the thesis · `[post-key]` on each
savor beat · nothing on connective tissue. This is the phase most tempted
to over-pause; the ration (≈3 per minute) still binds.

> `[verify]` claim SUPPORTS the payoff, it is not the payoff)*
