# P4 — THE MIDPOINT PIVOT

> Self-contained generation guide. Consume ONLY: the INJECTION block, the
> P3 ledger, and this file. Every line answers: which structural node,
> which retention duty, what scale. Lines that answer none get cut.

## Function

The hinge. A false peak (things look solved — they aren't) or a false
collapse (things look lost — they aren't) that ends the exploratory mode,
elevates the stakes, and flips the video from "examining a question" to
"racing a consequence". Structurally, this phase CURES the mid-video sag:
the reversal and the mid-video rehook are ONE event, so the viewer is
re-justified by the story itself, not by a bolted-on tease. Anticipation
peaks here; the planted head-fake dies here.

## Beat template

### Signpost IN (phase start)

- One explicit line telling the viewer the ground is about to move:
  "Here's where everything you've just seen flips." Auditory handrails
  are a feature, not a spoiler — the ear needs to know a turn is coming
  to feel it land.

### The reversal (the pivot's center)

- `[pre-key]` — the pause BEFORE the reveal is scripted, mandatory, and
  the only `[pre-key]` this phase gets.
- `[REVERSAL_LINE]` — then the flip, in the dramatic gear: **sentence
  length CONTRACTS** to short, punchy runs (drop to 4–9 words for 2–4
  sentences; the contraction must be measurable against the phase before).
- **The head-fake resolves INTO the reversal**: the obvious answer
  offered straight earlier is now demolished by the true mechanism —
  "It was never about X. It's Y." The viewer feels they earned the truth
  because they briefly believed the fake.
- **A4: the reversal IS the mid-video rehook.** Do not write a separate
  rehook line; the re-justification is the flip itself.

### Recontextualization (immediately after the reversal)

- **The ring token flips meaning: same object, opposite significance** —
  the strongest single move in the whole architecture (the receipt stops
  being proof of purchase and becomes a standing liability; the trophy
  becomes the debt). Quote the line into the ledger.
- Visual register SHIFTS on screen simultaneously — new palette, new
  scale, or new lens (rooms → a single object in macro; charts → faces).
  Scripted, not implied: write the visual direction.

### Stakes elevation (phase back half)

- Raise what's at risk in **concrete numbers**, not adjectives. The
  question is no longer "what's true?" but "what does it cost to keep
  being wrong?"
- Drive to the low point (the "all is lost" beat at full runtime), then
  exit on a signpost into reflection: "So what do you actually do with
  that?"

## MANDATORY: Glass alternation — ratio ≈ 50/50, fused

The pivot fuses the engines: the reversal is REFLECTION delivered with
the force of ACTION. Alternate line-by-line — a concrete image, then what
it now means, then the next image. Neither engine may run more than two
consecutive sentences here.

## MANDATORY: tricolon/anaphora — ZERO new figures

No new tricolon. No anaphora recurrence. This phase's rhetoric is the
contraction itself — short declaratives are the figure. Any triad here
steals thunder from the close. (Exception: the demolition line may echo
the head-fake's own wording to kill it — "Spend less. Want less. It was
never about less." — a subverted echo, not a new arc.)

## HARD GATE: attribution-first

The reversal's mechanism must stand on VERIFIED ground — the flip is the
one claim the video cannot afford to hedge. If the true mechanism's
evidence carries `[verify]`, the script is not ready to write; resolve the
verification first. Source before assertion everywhere, as always.

## Counterpoint: visual register shift

This phase's counterpoint IS the shift: the narration flips the meaning
while the screen flips the register. If the visuals continue unchanged
through the reversal, the pivot reads as a paragraph, not an event.

## Pause marks

Exactly one `[pre-key]` (before the reversal line). One `[post-key]`
after the recontextualization settles. Nothing else. Zero CTA of any kind
inside this phase.
