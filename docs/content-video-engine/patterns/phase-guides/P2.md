# P2 — THE ENGINE

> Self-contained generation guide. Consume ONLY: the INJECTION block, the
> P1 ledger, and this file. Every line answers: which structural node,
> which retention duty, what scale. Lines that answer none get cut.

## Function

The catalyst and the debate. The event or fact that makes the question
urgent lands here as a story beat, the obvious answer gets tried and found
wanting, and the first macro loop closes on a partial answer that opens a
bigger one. Anticipation (the "where is this going" charge) starts its
long climb here and must not peak before the midpoint.

| Runtime | P2 window | Density targets |

| 8 min | 1:00–2:15 | 2–3 closes · 4–6 new-info beats |

## Beat template

### The catalyst (phase start, first ~60s of the phase)

- The inciting fact/event arrives as a **micro story loop**: setup →
  tension → resolution, closed inside 30–60 seconds, with the next loop's
  question opened in the same breath. Chronology and concrete nouns carry
  it — this is anecdote, not exposition.
- **New-information cadence starts: something genuinely new every
  15–30 seconds.** An old fact re-phrased does not count.
- Every beat connects by BUT or THEREFORE — never AND-THEN. If two
  adjacent beats could swap positions without damage, one of them is
  filler.

### The head-fake (early-mid phase) — MANDATORY

- `[HEAD_FAKE_LINE]` — offer the obvious answer **straight**, with no
  wink: "So the fix is obvious, right? X." The audience must be allowed
  to believe it. This is the misdirection that resets anticipation and
  pays off at the midpoint pivot, where it is demolished. Reserve the
  demolition — do not undercut the fake here.

### Positional anchor A3 + foreshadow F2 (~10% of runtime; 3:00 @30min)

- **A3 rehook**: one template-family line ("But here's where it gets
  weird…") re-justifying the next stretch against a real open question.
- **F2**: the second sighting of the F1 promise — the payoff teased
  again, WITHOUT delivering any of it. One construction may legitimately
  serve both duties in one line; at short runtimes it should.

### The debate (mid-late phase)

- The obvious answer is **tried and found wanting** — as a gap (an
  action or example whose result violates expectation), never as a
  lecture ("that doesn't work because…"). Show the play failing.
- Point ordering: lead with strong-but-not-best evidence. The best
  evidence is BANKED for the unit before the midpoint — the retention
  peak gets the peak material.
- Ring token: touched once — re-sighted, unresolved, meaning unchanged.

### Macro close + dip + optional CTA (phase end)

- **Macro loop 1 closes** on a partial answer that opens the bigger
  question (the viewer gets paid AND leans in).
- Immediately after: **one breathing dip** — 2–3 beats of visual room
  tone, no narration push. Intentional dips beat accidental sag.
- **The ONLY permitted mid-video CTA slot in the entire video** sits
  here: inside the 15–30 seconds after the macro payoff, one soft ask
  maximum, and only if `{FORMAT}` warrants it. Skipping it is the
  default. Never ask before value has been delivered.
- Exit on a transition signpost into the gap phase ("that raises the
  real question…").

## MANDATORY: Glass alternation — phase ratio ≈ 70/30 anecdote

The catalyst and debate run as action; after each micro-loop close, ONE
reflection sentence extracts what it meant ("He's not reckless. He's
running the play he was handed."), then the next loop opens. Mark each
reflection dab. Two consecutive reflection sentences = you are lecturing;
two loops with no reflection = you are listing.

## MANDATORY: tricolon/anaphora placement

- ONE momentum tricolon is licensed at the catalyst or the debate's
  turn — three parallel strokes that accelerate ("earn more, upgrade,
  repeat"). No other triads.
- If the anaphora phrase debuted in P1, it recurs here ONCE with an
  evolved tail. Constant opening, changing end — the change carries the
  meaning. Do not resolve it.

## HARD GATE: attribution-first

Source before assertion on every claim, no exceptions. New figures enter
with their source in the same sentence. Unverifiable claims carry
`[verify]` and cannot anchor the catalyst or the macro close.

## Counterpoint: contextual mapping

Visuals carry the archive — data, documents, places. The voice carries
what the data cannot show: motive, cost, the narrator's read. A line that
captions its own visual fails.

## Pause marks

`[post-key]` after the macro-close payoff line. `[pre-key]` before the
head-fake's demolition is FORBIDDEN here (that pause belongs to the
pivot). Ration: ≤3 marks in this phase.

## Worked micro-example (3:00-scale)

> Start in his apartment. A suit rack that cost a paycheck. A watch box
> with a loan inside it. A television wider than the couch — and a rowing
> machine holding up laundry.
> *(catalyst as anecdote; concrete nouns; second callback token planted)*
> I used to risk-score portfolios for a bank. This room is a portfolio.
> Every position is a feeling he paid to stop having. `[post-key]`
> *(reflection dab — one sentence of meaning, then back to action)*
> So the fix is obvious, right? Spend less. Want less. Be more like her.
> *(head-fake planted straight — demolition reserved for the pivot)*
> But watch the account first — because the room is only half of it.
> Rent clears and the balance flinches. Bonus month is a memory by March.
> *(A3-family rehook + the debate: the play failing, shown not told)*
