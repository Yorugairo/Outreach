# P3 — THE GAP (the pattern unit)

> Self-contained generation guide. Consume ONLY: the INJECTION block, the
> P2 ledger, and this file. Every line answers: which structural node,
> which retention duty, what scale. Lines that answer none get cut.

## Function

The expectation-reality engine, and **the runtime knob for the whole
video**. This phase is built from a repeatable PATTERN UNIT: each unit
opens a gap (an action yields an unexpected result), forces a deeper and
riskier move, extracts meaning, and rehooks out. Longer videos add units;
they never stretch them.

## Geometry

| Runtime | P3 window | Units (2:00–2:30 each) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 30 min | 5:00–13:30 | 3–4 |
| 16 min | 3:00–7:15 | 2 |
| 8 min | 2:15–3:30 | 1 |
| < 5 min | one compressed unit | u1–u5 in ~25s, all five steps present |
| > 30 min | additional P3 blocks alternate with mini-pivots every ~12–15 min | scale by count |

Unit count ≈ `ceil((runtime_min − 9) / 2.5)`, minimum 1.

## Inputs

`{TOPIC}` `{VOICE}` `{PERSONA}` `{EVIDENCE}` + **P2 ledger** (open loops,
head-fake, ring token state, anaphora phrase, F-schedule).

## Ledger OUT (hand to P4)

```

ring_token:      touched ≤1× per unit, still unresolved
anaphora_arc:    recurrences quoted (≥3 across units at full length)
open_loops:      updated — nothing closed here except unit-local micros
```

## THE PATTERN UNIT (repeat N times)

| Step | Content | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **u1** | Anecdote run | 2–3 micro story loops, chronological, concrete nouns; new info every 15–30s; this is bait — it must keep raising unresolved questions or the information waits |
| **u2** | The gap opens | **BUT** — an expectation set in u1 is violated; the value charge flips (what looked good turns costly, what looked weak turns strong); the dramatic question sharpens |
| **u3** | The deeper move | **THEREFORE** — the gap compels a riskier action or a deeper explanation than the viewer expected; never a sideways AND-THEN |
| **u4** | Reflection beat | Meaning extraction, one to three sentences; `[post-key]` savor; **the anaphora phrase recurs with an evolved tail**; the emotional register SHIFTS here — every unit must land in a different feeling than it opened |
| **u5** | Rehook out | One template-family line re-justifying the next 60 seconds against the sharpened question → next unit's u1 (or the pivot) |

Per-unit obligations: one breathing dip (2–3 beats of room tone, usually
after u4) · dead air never exceeds ~12s without a visual event · ring
token touched at most once per unit, never resolved here.

## Across-unit placement rules

- **Best evidence lands in the FINAL unit before the pivot** — the
  retention peak gets the peak material. Spend the banked evidence there,
  nowhere earlier.
- **F3** (third and last sighting of the promise) at ~27% of runtime
  (~8:00 @30min). Tease, never deliver. After F3, zero re-promises — the
  next appearance of the promise is its payoff.
- Rehook density: the unit-exit rehooks are the baseline. Tightening to
  every 60–90s is a REMEDY applied on measured mid-video sag, not a
  default — extra rehooks with nothing real to re-justify read as noise.
- The head-fake from P2 stays alive: u2 gaps may wobble it, none may
  demolish it. The demolition belongs to the pivot.

## MANDATORY: Glass alternation — ratio ≈ 60/40, structured INSIDE the unit

The unit IS the alternation: u1–u3 are anecdote (bait), u4 is reflection
(insight). This is not optional texture — a unit without u4 is a shallow
list; a unit that is mostly u4 is a lecture. At full length, no unit may
skip any of the five steps. The ratio tightens toward reflection as the
phase approaches the pivot.

## MANDATORY: the anaphora arc (this phase owns it)

The anaphora phrase — constant opening, evolving tail — recurs in at
least THREE units' reflection beats at full runtime (once, in the single
unit, at short runtimes). Each recurrence's tail must show the argument
having MOVED ("He counts what lands. She counts what it costs."). Do not
resolve the arc; its final form belongs to the close. Tricolon budget in
this phase: zero — triads here dilute the two the video is allowed.

## HARD GATE: attribution-first

Source before assertion on every claim. Each unit's evidence enters with
its source in the same sentence. `[verify]`-flagged claims may support a
unit but may not BE a unit's gap (u2 must turn on verified ground).

## Counterpoint: subtext layering

The voice stops describing and starts meaning: visuals carry the surface
action; narration carries what it costs, who profits, what nobody in the
frame can see. A line that captions its visual fails harder here than
anywhere — this phase is where tautology bores viewers out.

## Worked micro-example (one compressed unit, 3:00-scale)

> Her place, on half the income. The first thing you notice is floor. A
> couch that gets sat on. A paper ledger. A jar her tips pass through on
> the way to somewhere that pays *her*.
> *(u1 — anecdote run, concrete, chronological)*
> But if "spend less" were the answer, half the country would be retired
> already.
> *(u2 — BUT: the gap opens; the head-fake wobbles, not demolished)*
> When you count other people's cash for eight hours a day, you stop
> worshipping it — and you start seeing what it actually *buys*.
> *(u3 — THEREFORE: the deeper move)*
> He counts what lands. She counts what it costs. `[post-key]`
> *(u4 — reflection; anaphora recurrence, evolved tail; register shifts
> envy → clarity; breathing dip after this line)*
> And this is where most people get the whole thing wrong.
> *(u5 — rehook out → pivot)*
