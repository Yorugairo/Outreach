# P6 — THE CLOSE

> Self-contained generation guide. Consume ONLY: the INJECTION block, the
> P5 ledger, and this file. Every line answers: which structural node,
> which retention duty, what scale. Lines that answer none get cut.

## Function

The ring seals, the thesis lands in its final form, and the ONE ask is
made inside its earned window. The end retroactively grades the whole
video (viewers remember the peak and the end — a weak final minute
discounts twenty-nine strong ones), and the assignment plants the reason
to come back. Revelation is this phase's product: the viewer leaves
changed and equipped.

## Geometry — true close is ABSOLUTE: 60–90s + 20s end screen

| Runtime | P6 window |
| --- | --- |
| 30 min | 26:00–30:00 (true close ~26:00–28:45, end screen final 20s) |
| 16 min | 14:00–16:00 |
| 8 min | 6:45–8:00 |
| serialized segment | ring echo + bridge only; **ZERO CTA** — the ask belongs to the video's true outro, not a segment boundary |

Nothing new after the CTA. Padding gets felt within 60 seconds; hard stop.

`{VOICE}` `{PERSONA}` `{FORMAT}` `{NEXT}` (the next video/segment's hook,

## Beat template

### The ring echo (phase start)

- **The opening image returns, transformed** — verbally AND visually:
  same frame family as 0:00, one register different (one light lower,
  one season later, the drawer now closing instead of opening). The
  viewer must be able to point at the echo.
- **The ring token — first planted, still open — closes LAST of all
  tokens**, in this beat or the next. Ring composition is what makes an
  audio-first structure feel complete without chapter cards: themes
  revisited in reverse order of arrival.

### The final triad (the emotional peak)

- **The thesis in three strokes — the video's LAST licensed tricolon —
  fused with the anaphora arc's final form**: the constant opening
  returns one last time and the third stroke either COMPLETES the
  pattern (gravity) or SUBVERTS it (the laugh that lands the point).
  Terminal stress on the third stroke's final word.
- The emotional peak of the entire video sits here, deliberately — peak
  placement at the end is what the memory keeps.

### The action window (15–30s AFTER the final payoff) — the ONE ask

- Exactly ONE call to action, structured on three pillars in order:
  1. **Value reinforcement** — name specifically what the viewer now
     has ("you can now price any object in your home in thirty
     seconds") — never generic "hope you enjoyed";
  2. **Clear direction** — subscribe FOR the named next thing, one
     verb, one target;
  3. **Future pacing** — one concrete picture of action→outcome ("next
     week, bring your number").
- "Like, comment, AND subscribe" is three asks and zero conversions.
  If `{FORMAT}` = serialized segment: SKIP this beat entirely.

### The assignment + outro template (phase end)

- **The viewer assignment**: the calculable take-home restated as
  tonight's action — empowerment is the standing close.
- Choose ONE outro template:
  - **Story Close** (default — ring-native): the narrative's last image
    carries the goodbye; no meta-talk.
  - **Cliffhanger Bridge** (serialized): the next segment's promise,
    planted with the same discipline as an opening promise — dated,
    calculable, one entity seed allowed.
- **Hard stop.** Zero new information after the CTA. End screen SOP:
  subscribe element + latest video + best-performing video, held the
  full 20 seconds — the narration is already over.

## MANDATORY: Glass alternation — ratio ≈ 40/60, closing on image

The close alternates one last time: the ring echo is ANECDOTE (an image,
a moment), the triad is REFLECTION (the meaning, final form). End on the
image side wherever possible — the last thing the ear keeps should be a
picture, not an abstraction.

## MANDATORY: tricolon/anaphora — the convergence point

This phase OWNS both figures' resolutions: the final tricolon and the
anaphora's last recurrence are ONE construction (see the triad beat).
Nothing else in the video may have resolved either. If the arc never
started (short runtime), the triad stands alone — still mandatory.

## HARD GATE: attribution-first

Still binding — including the bridge: any factual tease for the next
video carries its source or a first-person stake, never a bare claim.
No new `[verify]` flags may be introduced in the close.

## Counterpoint: ring symmetry

The visual close mirrors the visual open — the counterpoint here is
TIME: the same image means differently because of everything between.
Do not narrate the symmetry ("remember this from the start?" is banned);
let the frame do it.

## Worked micro-example (3:00-scale, serialized → Cliffhanger Bridge)

> **[return to the till plate — same frame as 0:00, drawer now closing]**
> So: two people, counting money. He counts what came in. She counts
> what it costs to keep. `[post-key]`
> *(ring echo — opening image transformed; anaphora arc closes with its
> final evolved tail)*
> Rule one: read the second line of the receipt.
> *(ring token — first planted — closes LAST)*
> Rule two is what she does with the money the second line never claims —
> and it isn't "spend less." Spending less is how you stay exactly where
> you are; she'd tell you that herself. One number, three dials — risk,
> reward, and time — and it moves your retirement date more than your
> salary ever will. That's next.
> *(Cliffhanger Bridge = next segment's dated promise; the triad rides
> the bridge; ZERO CTA — serialized segment)*
