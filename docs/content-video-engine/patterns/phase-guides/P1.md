# P1 — THE OPEN

> Self-contained generation guide. Consume ONLY: the INJECTION block and
> this file. Do not assume any other document exists. Every line you write
> must answer three questions: *which structural node am I serving, which
> retention duty am I discharging, and at what scale does my device repeat?*
> A line that answers none gets cut.

## Function

Plant the story's instability as **people**, open a gap the viewer needs
closed, sell a dated promise, and draw the map without spoiling the
territory. Roughly a third to half of the audience decides whether to stay
during this phase; if it fails, no later phase runs. Emotional targets:
visual+verbal stun inside 2 seconds; full captivation ("I am watching
this") by 0:15.

## Geometry — ABSOLUTE, not proportional

| Runtime | P1 window |
| --- | --- |
| 30 min | 0:00–1:30 |
| 16 min | 0:00–1:15 |
| 8 min | 0:00–1:00 |
| < 5 min | 0:00–0:35 — **compress beats, never drop them** |

At short runtimes systems MERGE rather than stack: one sentence may be the
promise, the first rehook, and the ring-token plant at once. Never cut the
promise, the paradox, or the token to save time.

## Inputs (injection slots)

- `{TOPIC}` — one claim cluster, one dramatic question.
- `{VOICE}` — the voice profile block (register, humor, never-list).
- `{PERSONA}` — narrator biography + standing theses; used for the
  biography-as-twist slot and the lens line.
- `{EVIDENCE}` — verified figures with sources; the open may use at most
  one or two, always attributed.
- `{RING_TOKEN}` — **choose it here if not injected**: one concrete,
  filmable object that can open the video and close it transformed
  (a receipt, a shipping container, a signature). The whole video's ring
  hangs on this choice.
- `{FORMAT}` — standalone | serialized segment | answer-to-a-channel.

## Ledger OUT (hand to P2)

```
ring_token:        <object> — PLANTED (quote the planting line)
F1_promise:        <exact promise text> — dated/calculable — pays off at 60–70% of runtime
open_loops:        L1 <the dramatic question> · macro-1 <the first sub-question>
anaphora_phrase:   <candidate constant opening, if debuted>
head_fake_reserved:<the obvious-but-wrong answer P2 will offer straight>

```

## Beat template

### Beat 1 — MICROHOOK (0:00–0:03)

- `[MICROHOOK_LINE]` — one sentence, present tense, concrete, the
  surprising word LAST (terminal stress). This line states something whose
  expected consequence the next line will violate — the gap opens here.
- Visual: the plate is already moving at frame one. Never black, never a
  logo, never a greeting. The classical pre-opener pause is bent into a
  **visual-only breath**: the image breathes ~0.5–0.8s before the first
  word. No cold silence.
- Counterpoint rule (irony): the image must NOT illustrate the line — it
  tensions it. Calm claim over an anomalous image, or the inverse.

### Beat 2 — QUICK RETURN (0:03–0:08)

- `[QUICK_RETURN_LINE]` — pay the microhook immediately, **and pay it
  wrong**: the answer violates the expectation the first line set.
  Follow with `[post-key]` so the settle-pause lands ON the 8-second
  decision boundary — the viewer decides during the breath after the
  paradox, not during throat-clearing.
- By 0:08 the viewer knows what the video is about — established by
  paradox, never by announcement. Banned: greetings, "in this video,"
  atmosphere-setting, channel talk.

### Beat 3 — THE WORLD OPENS (0:08–0:30)

- The **anecdote engine** starts: chronological, concrete, sequential
  bait that raises unresolved questions. Characters enter as
  **archetypes-in-specific-settings** (the banker in Manhattan, the
  budtender in Denver) — universal experience in a unique expression,
  never demographics. This is the story's Weakness & Need planted as
  people: the instability the whole video exists to resolve.
- **"You" enters here** — direct second-person, converting spectator to
  participant.
- `[BIOGRAPHY_TWIST]` (optional slot) — if the narrator's identity is
  part of the hook, it lands AFTER the paradox, as a twist, never before.
- Stakes named by ~0:25: target + transformation + what's at risk.
- Sentence mechanics fully active: one idea per sentence, active
  subject-verb-object, 10–15 word average in a wave of long-then-punchy.

### Beat 4 — MINI-PAYOFF + THE DATED PROMISE (0:30–0:60)

- **Deliver real value FIRST**: a genuine partial answer, proof the video
  pays, before asking the viewer to stay.
- Then `[pre-key]` and `[PROMISE_LINE]` — the promise with a **date or
  number**: "…and in the next N minutes you'll calculate yours." One line
  doing three jobs: the retention promise, the first macro loop's setup,
  and **rehook anchor A1 (~0:30)**.
- **TRICOLON LICENSED HERE (mandatory placement):** the promise or its
  setup is the phase's one thesis-grade line — build it as three strokes
  (setup, extension, resolution — or three negations: "isn't X, isn't Y,
  isn't Z"). This is the sentence the viewer should be able to repeat.
  A phonetic anchor (alliteration/assonance) is allowed here and nowhere
  else in the phase.
- The promise must be **calculable or checkable** — something the viewer
  can verify against their own life or the world.

### Beat 5 — THE MAP, NOT THE TERRITORY (0:60–0:90)

- Signpost the journey without spoiling it: tease the WHAT, hold the HOW
  ("three rules — the first is hiding on every receipt you've thrown
  away"). Never a table of contents.
- Name the goal and the **opponent — always a mechanism** (an incentive
  structure, a compounding cost, a play someone was handed), never a
  villain.
- **Rehook anchor A2 (~1:00)**: one line from the template families —
  "But here's where it gets weird…" / "This is where most people get it
  wrong…" — re-justifying the next 60 seconds against a REAL open
  question, not noise.
- `{RING_TOKEN}` must be planted by the end of this beat if it wasn't
  planted earlier. Quote the planting line into the ledger.
- **Context-dump ban:** any principle stated here must already be cashed
  into an object or a number within one sentence. History and theory wait
  until something is working on screen.

## MANDATORY: Glass alternation — phase ratio ≈ 80/20 anecdote

This phase runs the anecdote engine almost throughout. Exactly ONE
reflection dab is licensed (usually the persona-lens line in Beat 3 or 4:
one sentence of meaning extraction, then back to action). All-anecdote
across the whole video reads as a shallow list; all-reflection reads as a
lecture — the alternation starts here and never stops for the rest of the
video. State the dab you used in a comment.

## HARD GATE: attribution-first

Every factual claim opens with its source BEFORE the assertion ("The New
York Comptroller reported X" — never "X, according to…"). A claim you
cannot attribute carries `[verify]` and may NOT sit in the microhook or
the promise line. No exceptions; a script violating this gate fails the
phase regardless of quality elsewhere.

## Pause marks

`[post-key]` after the paradox (Beat 2) and after any line that must
settle. `[pre-key]` immediately before the promise. Nothing else — pauses
are rationed, roughly three per minute of narration maximum.

## Worked micro-example (~compressed, 3:00-scale segment)

> **[till plate, drawer sounds under; image breathes 0.6s]**
> She earns half of what he does. She retires first. `[post-key]`
> *(Beat 1+2 merged — microhook, terminal stress on "first"; quick return
> paid wrong)*
> He's a banker in Manhattan, counting a bonus. She's a budtender in
> Denver, counting the till in my dispensary — I own it, and I used to
> risk-score portfolios at JPMorgan.
> *(Beat 3 — archetypes-in-settings; biography-as-twist; anaphora
> candidate: "he counts / she counts")*
> The difference between them isn't discipline, isn't salary, isn't luck.
> `[pre-key]` It fits on one receipt — and in the next three minutes,
> you'll calculate yours. `[post-key]`
> *(Beat 4 — tricolon of negations; dated calculable promise = F1 = A1;
> ring token PLANTED: the receipt)*
> And the opponent here isn't a person. It's a play — the one he was
> handed: earn more, upgrade, repeat.
> *(Beat 5 — opponent as mechanism; map-not-territory)*
