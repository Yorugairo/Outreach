# FULL VIDEO MAP — McKee Extended to the YouTube Era

The calculus. The classical six-phase architecture (doc 32 / the research
blueprint) is the **integral** — where the story must be at every point. The
platform micro-rules (doc 31 / AOY) are the **differentials** — what happens
inside every 15–90 second interval to hold the viewer there. Neither works
alone; the operator's marked passages state both failure modes: *"relying
solely on surface-level retention tactics often causes long-form scripts to
collapse under narrative weight"* and the classical phases are vague about
HOW to fill their own minutes.

Operator direction (2026-08-24): *"Fill in the Macro 6 with ALL of the
expected micro hook / callback / CTA rules... we are extending Robert
McKee's foundational framework to the YouTube era, which requires even more
micro planning than his revolutionary system did at the time."*

Reference timeline: **30:00** (T3). Scaling law in §1. Sentence-layer
constants (the score) apply to every line of every phase — §2.

## 1. Geometry and the scaling law

- **Pinned absolute:** P1 THE OPEN is 60–90 seconds at every runtime —
  the attention ladder is physics, not proportion. P6's true close is 60–90
  seconds plus the 20-second end screen. The MIDPOINT pivot is pinned at
  **45–55% of runtime** at every length.
- **Elastic:** P3 (Gap units) is the primary runtime knob; P5 accepts a
  secondary gap block at T3. Unit count ≈ `ceil((runtime_min − 9) / 2.5)`,
  minimum 1.
- **Beyond 30 minutes** (operator rule: "repeat the process or increase
  pattern ratio"): alternate additional P3 blocks with mini-pivots every
  ~12–15 minutes; the single TRUE midpoint stays at global ~50%; one grand
  payoff, one ring.

| Phase | % of runtime | @30:00 | @16:00 | @8:00 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 OPEN | absolute | 0:00–1:30 | 0:00–1:15 | 0:00–1:00 |
| P2 ENGINE | to ~17% | 1:30–5:00 | 1:15–3:00 | 1:00–2:15 |
| P3 GAP | 17–45% | 5:00–13:30 | 3:00–7:15 | 2:15–3:30 |

| P6 CLOSE | final 60–90s + card | 26:00–30:00 | 14:00–16:00 | 6:45–8:00 |

## 2. Cross-phase constants (the constants of integration)

**Rehook anchors (positional, AOY):** A1 ≈ 0:30 · A2 ≈ 1:00 · A3 ≈ 3:00 ·
A4 = the midpoint pivot itself · plus one rehook closing every P3/P5 unit.
Five template families: "But here's where it gets weird…" / "What nobody
knew…" / "This is where most people get it wrong…" / "Let's fast-forward…" /
"But the real question is…". *Every 60–90s* is the diagnostic remedy for a
measured Slow Bleed, not the default.

**Foreshadow schedule (Grand-Payoff-Betrayal fix):** F1 = the dated promise
in the hook · F2 ≈ 10% (≈3:00 @30) · F3 ≈ 27% (≈8:00 @30) → payoff delivered
at 60–70% as natural culmination. Three references, one delivery, zero
re-promises after delivery.

**Callback ledger (ring = callbacks closed LIFO):** tokens are *planted* in
P1–P2, *touched* (re-sighted, unresolved) in P2–P3, *recontextualized* at
the P4 pivot (same object, opposite meaning — the strongest class), and
*closed in reverse order of planting* through P5–P6. The last token closed
is the first planted: the ring anchor (e.g. the receipt).

**CTA budget:** at most ONE micro-CTA, allowed only inside the 15–30s
action window after the first macro payoff (P2 tail); exactly ONE outro CTA
(P6). Nothing else, ever. "Like, comment, AND subscribe" is three asks and
zero conversions.

**Dopamine ladder mapping:** L1 stun 0–2s · L2 captivation by 0:15 ·
L3 anticipation runs P2→P4 (head-fakes reset it) · L4 validation in P5
(close loops COMPLETELY) · L5 affection = the falsifiable tell, competence
on record · L6 revelation = the close + assignment that brings them back.

**The score (every line, every phase):** 15–16 word average in a wave ·
one idea per sentence · active S-V-O · attribution-first · terminal stress ·
punctuation as performance notation · direct "you" · homophone kill ·
`[pre-key]`/`[post-key]` marks compiled per doc 37.

**Ceilings and floors:** dead air ≤12s (visual events) · ≤3 break tags per
TTS segment · one breathing dip per P3/P5 unit — intentional dips beat
pile-on · new information every 15–30s (relaxing to 30–45s only in P5
reflection stretches, never to zero).

**Counterpoint per phase:** irony (P1) → contextual mapping (P2) → subtext
layering (P3) → visual register shift (P4) → abstract synthesis (P5) →
ring symmetry (P6). A line that captions its own visual fails everywhere.

## 5. P3 — THE GAP (5:00–13:30, elastic: the pattern unit)

Classical: Expectation-Reality Gap engine · Opponent & Plan · subtext
layering · the anaphora arc runs across units.

**THE PATTERN UNIT (2:00–2:30, repeated 3–4× at reference):**

| Step | Content | Micro rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| u1 | Anecdote run | 2–3 STR micro loops; chronological; new-info 15–30s; concrete nouns |
| u2 | Gap opens | BUT — expectation violated; value charge flips; dramatic question sharpens |
| u3 | Deeper move | THEREFORE — the riskier, deeper action/explanation McKee's gap compels |
| u4 | Reflection beat | Glass meaning extraction; `[post-key]` savor; the anaphora phrase recurs with an evolved tail; emotional register SHIFTS here (Element 4) |
| u5 | Rehook out | template family line re-justifying the next 60s → next unit's u1 |

Across-unit placement rules: **best evidence lands in the final unit before
the pivot** (retention peak gets the peak material) · **F3** at ~27% (≈8:00)
· one breathing dip per unit · dead-air ceiling enforced per unit · Slow
Bleed remedy (rehooks to 60–90s) only on measured evidence · ring tokens
touched at most once per unit, never resolved here.

Scaling: this is the runtime knob. 8–10 min → 1 unit · 12–18 → 2–3 ·
20–30 → 3–5 · beyond 30 → more units + mini-pivots (§1).

QC: units countable and complete (all five steps) · anaphora in ≥3 units ·
best-evidence position honored · every unit shifts emotional register ·
zero units without a reflection beat (all-anecdote = shallow list).
