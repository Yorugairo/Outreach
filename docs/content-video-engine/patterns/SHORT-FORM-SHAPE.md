# SHORT-FORM SHAPE — the five positions and the enter each one takes

What the one-shot proved (2026-09-12, `projects/systems-and-blowups/normal-for-which-bridge`: a NEW short written
and built in one pass, pages only, no docks and no clips). Doc 51 gives the shape in narration
([51-THE-SHORTS-FORMAT.md](../51-THE-SHORTS-FORMAT.md) §51.2) and `gate_opening_structure.py` gates it as S01–S08.
This page adds the one thing neither of them names: **how the world ARRIVES at each position** — the `enter` the row
declares, or the one the compiler stamps for it.

Nothing here restates doctrine. The four numbers are 51.1; the chart-on-the-hook ruling is 51.2 (E44); the register of
every enter is `build_scene_timeline_f.py` `LEDGER_ENTERS`; the species each sentence may take is
[SPECIES-BY-SENTENCE.md](../SPECIES-BY-SENTENCE.md).

## The five positions

| # | Position | What the sentence does | `enter` | Bound by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **APEX READ** | the hook's claim, on the page that proves it | `axes` (declared) | S01 · S02 (E44) |
| 2 | **INSTANCES** | the same mechanism again, new variables | `axes` (stamped) | S03 · S04/J51 · M31 |
| 3 | **THE TURN** | the first page returns and is read against itself | `spiral` (declared) | S06 · M05/E69 |
| 4 | **THE COST** | what the mechanism costs, as its own page | `axes` (stamped) | S03 · J50 |
| 5 | **THE RING** | the hook's question, asked of the page again | `spiral` (declared) | S05 · E56 |

## 1. The APEX READ — `enter=axes`

The charcoal page lands on its ground at frame 0 with its title, its scale, its ticks and its axes drawn, and the DATA
draws from the first frame (P53 T1; `enter=axes` in `LEDGER_ENTERS`). On the one-shot's own frames
(`build-short-axes/self-watch/axes/opening.png`) 0.00 s carries the page and no data, the line starts at 0.30 s and is
complete by 2.0 s.

- **S01** (`gate_opening_structure.py`, 51.2): the claim, spoken and on screen, inside 0:00–0:03. A roll-out spends
  those three seconds arriving, which is why the hook's page is the one position that never takes the default.
- **S02** (E44, the gate condition 2026-09-12): the mechanism is ON the page by 0:10 — an axes page satisfies it at 0:00.
- The page's own clock moves the annotation window with it: the line finishes at 3.0 s, so M11 puts the light on the
  word the light illustrates, not 4 s later where the roll-out used to put it.

## 2. The INSTANCES — `enter=axes`, stamped

One page per instance, each arriving **with ink** because it follows a world-taking transition (a suck or a melt). The
author declares nothing: `stamp_transition_pages` stamps `enter=axes` on the page AFTER one and `exit=cut` on the page
UNDER one, and the build prints each stamp (P53 T2/T6, R26-60).

- **S03**: at least two `[new]`/`[catalyst]` instances between the mechanism and the ring.
- **S04 / J51**: the instances share a spine — the same mechanism with different variables, never a pile (51.2, G-g).
- **M31** (`gate_motion_density.py`, from `measure_stage_gaps.py`): an empty stage that is spoken over FAILs. Measured
  before the stamps: 6.2 s empty, 8.9% of a 69.8 s short, the suck and the melt each 3.1 s and each spoken over.
  After: 0.0%.

## 3. The TURN — `enter=spiral`

The page that opened the short comes back, **already drawn**, and unwinds from its point: no roll, no soak, no ink, no
re-build (E25; `spiral` in `LEDGER_ENTERS`). It is the same world returning, so it takes no dip
(`SIGNATURE_ENTERS`) — and because nothing re-draws, the argument itself has to move: the one-shot takes its two loads
one at a time (`build_to` → `figure` → `span` → `figure`).

- **S06**: a rehook every 30 s. On a 45–60 s short the turn IS that rehook.
- **M05 / E69**: a held frame is legal while it LIVES. A pages-only short has no docks to break a long hold, so a
  20 s+ page that only sits is the failure this position exists to prevent (the v1 table held the bridge 29 s).

## 4. The COST — `enter=axes`, stamped

Its own page, entered with ink after the melt, cut out again. It is an INSTANCE of the same mechanism, not a second
mechanism: the load has a price, and the price is the same claim in dollars.

- **S03** counts it with the instances; **J50** (one mechanism) is what forbids it becoming a new argument.
- What the one-shot deleted here is the lesson: a `span` on the reflect sentence was cut because M28 caught its label
  under the figure's own pill, and "nothing defaulted" is not a claim about a stretch of time. Motion for motion's
  sake is not a position.

## 5. The RING — `enter=spiral`

The opening page returns a second time, already drawn, and is asked the hook's own question (`retitle` rewrites the
title by hand, a `figure` re-states the old number, and the `ring` marks today's).

- **S05**: the ring lands in the last 20% and returns to the MECHANISM — the token and the claim's stems, not a phrase.
- **E56**: a ring circles a number or a point on a chart, and nothing else. Its flag chip names the datum.

## Proposing the rows for a position

`lint_species_choice.py --propose` reads every sentence's ACT against the map and offers a draft row per available
species for any sentence with an act and nothing firing — anchored on a PHRASE, targeted off the series' own `facts`.
It is a **proposer**: it writes no shot table and chooses nothing by count, because Stage 7 is authored
([PIPELINE.md](../PIPELINE.md), "There is no allocator"). It does not know a page's own draw clock, so a proposal
inside the apex read's first 3 s is usually wrong — keep what belongs, delete the rest.
