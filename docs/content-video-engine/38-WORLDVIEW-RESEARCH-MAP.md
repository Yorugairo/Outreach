## Framework retrieval — load only what the episode needs

### Accretive burn and funded runway

Meaning: funding sustains a credible build long enough to earn a valuable
position; immediate earnings exceeding spending is not required.
Examples for investigation: IonQ's technology/manufacturing build and
APLD/WULF's infrastructure build. Historical examples are not recommendations.
Look for: funding availability and restrictions, leadership, milestones,
productive capacity, customer conversion and dilution.
Challenge: funding ends before meaningful progress, or spending fails to create
a position customers value. Do not impose a universal backlog requirement.
Source: [cross-cluster research §1](../research/markets/sovereign-compute/04_Research_Deep_Storage_Cross_Cluster.md).
Its numeric screening thresholds are historical research choices, not universal
voice gates. Operator corrections override narrower or absolute formulations.

### Sailboat versus wind — leadership changes the position

Meaning: distinguish deliberate financing, execution and moat-building from
merely riding sector demand. Examine decisions, not charisma.
Examples for investigation: APLD/WULF financing and deployment milestones.
Challenge: favorable conditions explain the results while management decisions
fail to improve the business's ability to compete through a reversal.
Source: [APLD/WULF theoretical framework](../research/markets/sovereign-compute/12_Applied_Digital_and_TeraWulf_Deep_Analysis.md).

### Sovereign compute and physical bottlenecks

Meaning: compute growth depends on power, sites, cooling, memory and interconnects;
domestic control and policy affect funding, access and ownership.
Examples for investigation: energized data-center capacity and optical/network
links. A company mentioned in a dossier is not automatically a bottleneck.
Challenge: substitution, new supply, delayed demand or policy changes weaken
the claimed scarcity or the company's ability to capture its value.
Sources: the two dossiers above; retrieve the relevant sector with
`python content/video_engine/scripts/docs_find.py "<sector or company>"`.

### Downturns can transfer control

Meaning: a strategically useful asset can outlast its owner's financing.
Stronger buyers may secure assets, capacity or bargaining power in distress.
Operator scenario examples: WULF/APLD/CRDO/NBIS, not confirmed acquisition
targets. Do not treat these distinct businesses as financially interchangeable.
Challenge: debt, contracts, regulation, alternative funding, asset economics
or unwilling sellers defeat the proposed bargain. Government funding can
preserve a rival's independence as well as facilitate consolidation.
Source: doc 30 §10 and current episode-specific primary-source research.
No proposed drawdown percentage, unchanged asset valuation or acquisition
approval becomes fact through inclusion in this framework.
