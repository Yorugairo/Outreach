# Answer Script — "Steel and Paper" (response to Bravos: "History is About to Be Made")

First full production run of the script-pattern kit (P1→P6 ledger chain),
in the answer format: the agreeing-but-contrarian next click. Target video:
Bravos Research, "History is About to Be Made... (Emergency Update)"
(https://youtu.be/Jw8ykhoOVBQ, 14:20, watched 2026-08-24, transcript on
file). Runtime: **8:00 (T1)** — geometry per the kit: P1 0:00–1:00 · P2
1:00–2:15 · P3 2:15–3:30 (one unit) · P4 3:30–4:30 · P5 4:30–6:45 · P6
6:45–8:00.

Their argument, honestly compressed: semis +300% since April while the
hyperscalers who fund them sit flat → Gartner hype cycle → capital share
of GDP crossed the historic 7% peak threshold (AI ≈ 8%) → but the pop
needs a trigger, and the trigger is the Fed back above 5.5%, where the
borrowing began. Near term they're constructive on hyperscalers; their
CTA sells strategy calls.

Our answer thesis (the registered relocation): the cycle is real, the
threshold is real, the tripwire is real — but the bubble's address is
wrong. The fragility is not in the sold-out builders; it is in the paper
wrapped around them — a passive index carrying 20% of its weight in one
bet. Steel and paper.

lens, T11 institutional cage, T12 conditional contrarian, memory-as-trade)
· ENTITY_SEED = Bravos Research (the answer target IS the seed) ·
