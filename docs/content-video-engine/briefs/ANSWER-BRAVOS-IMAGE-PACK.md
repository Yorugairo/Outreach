# Image Prompt Pack — "Steel and Paper" (GPT image generation)

Companion to [ANSWER-BRAVOS-HYPE-CYCLE.md](ANSWER-BRAVOS-HYPE-CYCLE.md).
World/metaphor plates only — **every data chart is built in-house from the
registered figures** (charts are evidence; generated images may never
carry data). Counterpoint doctrine governs the pack: the plate carries the
world, the narration carries the mechanism — no plate may illustrate its
line literally.

## Global style block (paste as the prefix of every prompt)

> Painterly editorial illustration, cinematic 16:9, muted documentary
> palette: warm cream #F4E6C7, deep slate #25313C, archival blue #1769C2,
> with restrained accents of teal #178C83 and coal-fire coral #ED6A4A.
> Soft directional key light, heavy atmosphere, visible brush texture,
> shallow depth of field. Composition leaves generous negative space on
> one side for later data overlay. Absolutely no text, no numbers, no
> letters, no logos, no watermarks, no real people's faces.

**Hard rules for every generation:**
- No readable text anywhere — engraved paper and documents must be
  illegible scrollwork and texture, never legible words.
- No real-person likenesses (no Karp, no executives) and no company
  logos (no Uber, Microsoft, Nvidia marks). Archetypes and objects only.
- Keep the ring-token spike IDENTICAL across P-01, P-04b, and P-06a/c:
  same hand-forged iron spike, square-shanked, hammer-marked head, dark
  oiled wood desk. Generate P-01 first and reuse it as the reference
  image for the other spike plates.

## The plates
