# The page is our ground — how props-compose works in OUR style, 2026-09-04

Wealth Logic composes props on empty white. We are not going to adopt white: their
ground *is* their identity, and copying it would trade ours away. The question is what
does that job in our visual language.

**We already built it. The ledger page is our white.**

---

## The move

E22 fixed the page: cream ground, charcoal filling to the deckle, the line, then the
chart. We have been treating it as **a chart surface**. It is a **working surface** — a
ledger is where a thing is worked out, which is the channel's whole posture.

> **Metaphors are drawn ON the page, in ink, on the cream. Then the page becomes the
> chart.**

That does the job their white ground does, using the asset that is most ours, and it
makes the metaphor→evidence transition *continuous instead of a cut*: the same sheet
carries the argument and then carries the proof.

## Why this is better than copying them, not just different

| | their white | our cream page |
|---|---|---|
| identity | generic, any explainer channel | the channel signature (E22) |
| metaphor → chart | two separate compositions | **one surface transforming** |
| transition | a cut | ROLL 0.7 s + SAVOR 0.8 s — **starts inside the acoustic gap**, builds under the next sentence (M13) |
| the chart's arrival | pasted over the ground | drawn out of the ground it was already standing on |
| E25's "no chart survives a plate change" | a rule you must obey | **structurally impossible to break** — there is no plate change |

Their arrangement has an inherent seam between the argument and the evidence. Ours does
not have to.

## The three tiers, and what each is for

**WORLD (woodblock vox newsprint — 71 approved plates).** Establishing beats and the big
emotional images. Parallax-capable, expensive to make, reusable across episodes through
the plate library. **Three to five per episode, not thirteen.** They stop being the
default and become the thing you spend.

**PAGE (cream ledger).** The working majority. Metaphors as inked objects, then charts.
Cheap: a prop is an ink object, reusable forever, and it composes — one page can hold a
gate, a figure and a stamp the way `frame_0026` holds pens, cash and calendars.

**ACTOR (@Mike, full colour).** Lives on world plates. **He does not stand on the
page** — a colour cartoon on an ink ledger is a register collision. On the page his
presence is *hands*: the record-document species already strokes a highlighter in time
with the narrator's words, which is a hand working the ledger. `build-f/ledger-hands.html`
is the existing proof.

## What has to be built

`ledger_page.py` currently emits five variants — `line`, `bars`, `race`, `decline`,
`progress` — **all of them charts.** The page cannot yet hold a drawn object.

1. **A non-chart page variant** (`object`), where BUILD draws an inked prop instead of
   bars. Same ROLL/SAVOR/FIELD/PUNCH/BUILD clock, same deckle, same focus at 7.4 s.
2. **A prop library at the page tier** — transparent ink objects, channel-walled and
   indexed like plates: toll gate, empty chair + cold cup + bill, crate stamped with a
   future year, tipping scale, a stack against a small figure. **These are the cheap,
   permanent assets.** Every episode adds a few and inherits all the rest.
3. **One page carrying object → chart**, so the metaphor and its proof are the same
   sheet. This is the piece that makes the whole thing pay.
4. **Shot-table briefs name pose + props + ground**, never a place. "Toll gate" is a
   prop, not a location.

## What we keep that no reference has

Evidence discipline. Every figure on those pages traces to a series and a fetch date.
Wealth Logic's charts are drawn to be persuasive; ours are built from FRED and Treasury
and fail loudly when a source is unreachable. That is the part not to trade away for
anybody's look.
