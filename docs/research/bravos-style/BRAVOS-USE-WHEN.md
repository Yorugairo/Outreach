# Bravos moves by sentence act: a USE-WHEN guide (explorer, 2026-09-23)

This guide has one entry for every TYPE, ACTION, EFFECT and RECIPE in `BRAVOS-VOCABULARY-HARVEST-v2.md`: 171 entries. The ids, statuses and video counts come from that file.

**Rulings since this guide was written** (`docs/portable/OPERATOR-RULINGS.md`, E99, all 2026-09-23). Where one overturned a "don't", the entry now states the ruling's condition and cites it by id.
- **s99**: "A highlight that TRAVELS along a length (a relit stretch running down a line) or BLINKS counts as motion; a light that comes on as everything else STOPS is a punctuation beat" (a light that sits stays 0 events, s91).
- **s100**: an area form is truthful when "(a) every area is drawn in true proportion to its value ... and (b) the figures the claim turns on are WRITTEN on the page"; E53 s1's hierarchy "becomes GUIDANCE on which form reads fastest, not a refusal list".
- **s101**: "A bar may be filled with EQUAL tiles naming who is in it (logos, names) when the bar is ONE value, the tiles carry no value of their own (equal height, never sized), and the bar's total is written on the page".
- **s102**: a second axis, and an inverted one, "Allowed when: (a) the claim is co-movement or lead/lag; (b) each axis names its unit, and an inverted axis says "inverted" on the page; (c) each axis's tick labels are coloured with their own series."
- **s103**: H-3 keeps its share certificate: "The still stands as generated; the re-roll owed by HOST-NOTES-H.md is withdrawn."
- **s104** (+ amendments): a ledger page holds two, then up to FOUR panels; a panel "can RECEDE - scaled back, dimmed, softened behind the one in focus - and be BROUGHT BACK on its word"; "Focus is a COMPOSABLE state".
- **s105** (+ amendment): "A RECAST IS AN ARRIVAL"; for a rescale, extend or morph "THE TEST IS NARRATIVE, NOT GEOMETRIC".
- **s106**: "A prop's placement is AUTHORABLE"; the fit's findings "are WARNs in the build's report, not refusals".
- **s107**: "PROPS, PAGES AND CHARTS MORPH INTO EACH OTHER, BOTH WAYS", "MID-PAGE on a word".
- **s108**: a page's chrome "is a set of OBJECTS, not ink fixed in the page's space", "so the camera is free to push to the data".
- **s109**: "FORMS ARE NOT BANNED; HONESTY IS THE TEST": (1) schematics with no data; (2) the membership stack; (3) a ring on every vertex "when the sentence is about those vertices"; (4) pies and donuts, "including an extruded or exploded one", and the push onto the largest slice; (5) E53's form rules "are DEFAULTS that give way to the story when the honesty tests hold". "The broken cross-era axis (C13) awaits its own word."

Entries are grouped by the sentence's **act**. The act names are the ones in `docs/content-video-engine/SPECIES-BY-SENTENCE.md` (fable-p68): QUOTES, RANKS, COMPARES, DIVIDES, NAMES, EXPLAINS, TURNS, BREAKS, SPANS, SETS, RETRACTS, COUNTS. Four more rows from that file also get groups:
- the same data again (E58 verbs, its row 12);
- the camera (row 14);
- the bridge (a sentence with no act, E61 #2);
- the plate.

One gap: SPECIES-BY-SENTENCE has **no act for a sentence that projects** ("on track to reach", "could continue until 2027"). Its nearest act is COMPARES over time with `extend`, so the projecting moves sit in their own sub-group, 3b.

**Fields.** Each entry has:
- **act**
- **moment**: hook / setup / proof / turn / reveal / close
- **shape**: one number / two numbers / series / parts of a whole / before-after / a named thing
- **use**, one line
- **don't**, one line
- **ev**, 1-3 examples as `video-id mm:ss "quote"`

Narration quotes are kept under 15 words. `(screen)` marks on-screen text where the review gives no narration. `(G)` marks a Gemini-only timing. The BOOM (`jx3Ll-GJtMY`) quotes cannot be checked against any file on disk.

## 1. QUOTES: their claim, their words

**T42 Source document, passage highlighted, big figure over it**: TYPE · HAVE · n=1
- act: QUOTES · moment: proof · shape: one number
- use: the figure's authority is the document itself ("some estimates"), and one number carries the claim.
- don't: the figure is ours or derived (write it at its datum on a page instead); the page is unreadable at phone scale.
- ev: `nB1eXWQlW58` 03:20 (screen "$14.2 trillion" over the highlighted passage)

**T43 Quote card with a grayscale portrait**: TYPE · HAVE · n=1
- act: QUOTES · moment: turn · shape: a named thing
- use: a famous line changes the argument's register (a breath between history and now).
- don't: the quote is not attested; it would sit over a chart that is still being read.
- ev: `4AkB4c0tTfU` 07:44 "The market can stay irrational longer than you can stay solvent."

**A19 A card docks onto the plot at its date or peak**: ACTION · PARTIAL · n=4
- act: QUOTES · moment: proof · shape: a named thing + series
- use: a headline belongs to one date on the line, and the join IS the claim ("the warnings began here").
- don't: under E63 a card never READS over a ledger page's plot, so read it in its slot, then park it to a chip at the datum (C2). Bravos parks the card beside the plot in JPN 04:08.
- ev: `RUH3BPQ5fTo` 07:36 "The credit market began flashing warning signs in early 2000"; `4AkB4c0tTfU` 08:04 (screen: Retail Investors card joined to the latest peak); `jx3Ll-GJtMY` 00:44 (G, screen: news card inside the chart)

- ev: `1ZS5_txbOsc` 01:28.8 "Oil would spike to 150 or even $200 a barrel"; `4AkB4c0tTfU` 07:25 (screen: investor / sector tiles in a side cluster)

**A68 Hard cut from the chart to a speaker clip**: ACTION · HAVE · n=1

## 2. RANKS: who is biggest, the order of a set

**T2 Axis-less story bars (a pill over each bar, a glowing value badge)**: TYPE · HAVE · n=3
- act: RANKS · moment: proof · shape: two numbers (2-3)
- use: two or three magnitudes, each named, each spoken; the badge is the value.
- don't: more than four bars, or values that need a scale to be judged. E99 s96 caps the width (a bar is narrow).

**A25 A value badge lands on each bar on its word**: ACTION · HAVE · n=4

## 3. COMPARES over time: rose, fell, since

**T4 Divergence pair / side-by-side charts**: TYPE · HAVE · n=2
- act: COMPARES · moment: setup / proof · shape: series x2
- use: two markets or two eras, each on its own frame ("calm here, cracks there").
- don't: the two share a unit (overlay them on one scale).
- ev: `RUH3BPQ5fTo` 01:48 "the credit market … is beginning to show cracks"; `jx3Ll-GJtMY` 06:10 (G, screen: dot-com vs 1929 split)

**T5 Framed single line with a threshold rule**: TYPE · HAVE · n=3
- act: COMPARES · moment: proof · shape: series + one number
- use: a level is the claim ("highest since 2009", "above 4 %").
- don't: the rule is a value already on the chart (E53 addendum). A policy rate is a rule, not a series (E53 s5).
- ev: `RUH3BPQ5fTo` 02:24 "surged to the highest level since 2009"; `PWMhM2_dj3s` 00:50 (screen "4%"); `Jw8ykhoOVBQ` 02:43 (screen: dashed reference near the endpoint)

**T6 Neon multi-line**: TYPE · HAVE · n=6
- act: COMPARES · moment: proof · shape: series (3-6)
- use: several named series move together or apart, each tagged at its end.
- don't: all of them lit at once. Bravos draws the old cycles muted (BOOM 01:02).
- ev: `1ZS5_txbOsc` 16:45 "yields moving higher"; `4AkB4c0tTfU` 00:20 "But it's not all doom and gloom."; `nB1eXWQlW58` 05:20 (screen "Foreign Holdings of US Treasuries")

**T12 Annual columns with a dashed max rule and a ring**: TYPE · HAVE · n=1

- don't: a continuous quantity (E53 s3: that is a line).
