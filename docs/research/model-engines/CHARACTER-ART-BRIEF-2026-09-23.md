## First art review frame

Show a named fighter's head-and-shoulders at the intended 9:16 phone crop beside (1) a neutral clay mesh, (2) a three-quarter view, (3) an expression/stress view, and (4) one full-body costume view. A reviewer should recognize the fighter without a caption. Characterization can exaggerate head shape, brows, beard, torso and pose, but it must not collapse into a generic bald head plus a beard texture. The current `fighter-family-v1.blend` is a diagnostic rig only; its generic face, quiff and painted shorts fail this art test.

Source fight timing, contact choreography, the user-locked non-gory effect, and allegation attribution remain governed by the approved PRP and episode ledger. This brief only informs model identity and presentation.
