## Wealth-ratio reference: separate future concept

The useful promise is memorable simplicity: three roles for money, resilience and the ability to avoid forced selling. The opening attributes group wealth outcomes to an ancient allocation system; later passages treat fixed thirds as protection against virtually any failure. Those causal/absolute claims are not established by the video. Its devotional ending also serves a different audience promise than Money Physics.

Potential later response: test an old three-part allocation idea against modern risks using historically grounded storytelling and transparent scenario comparisons. Do not infer a uniform financial practice or cause of wealth for Jewish people, or sell33/33/33 as a universal optimum. Better rendering alone is not a differentiated argument.
