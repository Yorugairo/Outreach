# Animation Production Inference Map — reusable stylized fighters

*Cross-bundle intake synthesis · 2026-09-22 · source-grounded proposals, not primary-source verification, implementation, or approval*

## The question

How can the four anime production bundles and the rigging/combat bundle inform a repeatable 2D, 2.5D, and stylized-3D pipeline for recognizable fighter caricatures with distinctive identity add-ons, while keeping source technique, production inference, and speculative numbers visibly separate?

## Verdict up front

The reusable unit should be a **bounded caricature family + a small set of authored identity add-ons + a shared pose/contact contract**. The anime bundles support a layered workflow: a timed layout packet controls staging; 2D keys and FX carry graphic intent; 2.5D plates and camera projections provide depth; a stylized-3D rig supplies repeatable poses and contacts; final line, expression, and FX passes restore authored character. This intake does **not** approve a model, rig, proxy body, numeric default, likeness, or source citation.

All 20 entries below are `proposal, not implemented by this intake`. “Reported technique” records what the supplied research says. “Inference (this map)” is an operational translation, not a claim that the cited studio used the proposed implementation. “Unverified quantitative claims” are quarantined and must not become runtime constants, medical claims, or editorial assertions without a new evidence pass.

## Cross-cutting identity and acceptance contract

For the current knockout short, preserve native audio without time stretching and show contact → follow-through → fall → ground impact. Impact FX must follow actual contact/head snap; a key-pose label is not permission to cut early or replace the action with a hold.

1. **Recognizable fighter:** Start with a bounded family of body proportions and a named design contract; add a small number of stable silhouette, facial, prop/garment, or FX identity cues. Do not imply exact likeness from generic proxy geometry.
2. **Layer ownership:** Separate base mesh/rig, expression controls, line/shadow art, materials, FX, and camera. A material swap must not silently change proportions or identity.
3. **Proof order:** source-timed keys → rig/deformation stress → floor/contact diagnostics → rendered frame review → playable/decoded shot → editorial/visual approval. Keep generated output quarantined until operator approval.
4. **No invented physics:** studio pixel constants, Titan blast energy/temperature, rigid human injury, universal hold timings, universal solver convergence, and fixed hit-stop ranges remain `SOURCES-TO-VERIFY`, not runtime defaults or narration.
5. **Completion boundary:** The intake-review’s required next acceptance is one properly modeled stylized fighter with visible shoulders, elbows, hips, and knees under deformation stress, then a two-character source-timed exchange through floor contact. Proxy bodies do not count as character-art acceptance.
