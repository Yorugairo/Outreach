## 4. Things you didn't know, ranked by surprise

0. **Five days after "investigated for treason", the White House seated Lisa Su at the head table of its state dinner for Xi Jinping.** She was two seats from Trump, one of four business leaders at that table with Huang, Cook and Musk. (CONFIRMED, sources/36)
1. **AMD once licensed its x86 server design to a Chinese venture.** That was 2016, for $293M headline, and AMD recognized $198M of it in 2017-19. Three years later Washington blacklisted the venture alongside Sugon, a supercomputer maker with acknowledged military end uses. The Chinese partner, Hygon, later listed and was valued around $43B. (CONFIRMED; valuation PLAUSIBLE)
2. **To buy Xilinx, AMD promised Beijing it would keep selling Xilinx FPGAs to China.** Terms are "fair, reasonable, non-discriminatory". AMD reports to SAMR every six months and cannot ask to lift the promise before about 2028. The FPGA line is the product family this story is about. (CONFIRMED; the "no US-law carve-out" reading is PLAUSIBLE, so verify before airing)
3. **In 2020, AMD billed more revenue to China than to the United States** ($2.33B vs $2.29B). (CONFIRMED)
4. **Patel's own follow-up changed the story, and almost nobody reported it.** AMD told him it didn't sell to this firm and is investigating the sourcing. He then raised "bitstream compatible FPGAs not from AMD", meaning possible Chinese clones. (CONFIRMED as his post)
5. **The "treason" post also called pharma companies "similarly treasonous."** (CONFIRMED) This cuts against a framing of "he never says this". Air it for honesty.
6. **Two days after the treason call, AMD passed $1 trillion.** The stock rose 9.95% in the first session after the post, and the market ignored it. (price CONFIRMED; $1T PLAUSIBLE)
7. **Washington takes a cut of AMD's China AI chip sales.** It is a reported 15% on MI308. AMD shipped $390M of them to China in Q4 2025, and in Jan 2026 the US opened licences for newer chips. At the same moment an old radio chip stays walled off. (PLAUSIBLE / CONFIRMED mix)
8. **The US blacklisted China's own FPGA maker, Fudan Micro, in 2025.** The stated reasons were acquiring US items for military modernization and supplying Russia's military. A Chinese seller now advertises a Fudan RFSoC as "compatible" with the chip Patel named. (CONFIRMED / UNSOURCED)
9. **Puzhi itself says what the board is for at scale.** It lists phased-array radar, and its team phase-locked 7 boards with 56 channels, pitched for radar and direction-finding research. (CONFIRMED / PLAUSIBLE)
10. **About half of China's chips come in through Hong Kong.** It was 52% in Jan-May 2026, up from a third a decade ago. US law has treated Hong Kong as China since 2020-12-23, and BIS's own page warns that HK is used to route around US rules. (PLAUSIBLE / CONFIRMED)
11. **The PLA buys US-designed chips, Xilinx among them, through middlemen.** CSET found 97 AI chips in 24 of its 2020 contracts, nearly all US-designed. (CONFIRMED)
