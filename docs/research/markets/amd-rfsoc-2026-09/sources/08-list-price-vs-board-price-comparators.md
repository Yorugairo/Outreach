## Reading (inference, labelled)
A complete AMD-programme board carrying a Gen-3 RFSoC (a close sibling of the ZU47DR: the ZU48DR adds SD-FEC) sells to universities at $2,499, about 1/11 of the DigiKey qty-1 price of the bare chip ($27,686.12). So the qty-1 catalogue price is not what large or favoured buyers pay. This supports the general plausibility of Patel's "$4-5k at volume" claim (which is itself unsourced) and weakens the inference that a sub-list price in China proves wrongdoing by AMD. It does not tell us AMD's actual volume pricing (not public; not found).
