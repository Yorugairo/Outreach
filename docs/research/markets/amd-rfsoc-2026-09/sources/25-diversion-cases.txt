== Operation Gatekeeper (DOJ, 2025-12-08) via Arnold & Porter blog: https://www.arnoldporter.com/en/perspectives/blogs/enforcement-edge/2025/12/doj-shutdown-of-major-china-linked-ai-tech-smuggling-network
"at least $160 million worth of restricted Nvidia AI chips" (H100/H200) to mainland China and Hong Kong
Defendants "conspired with employees of a Hong Kong-based logistics company"
Network "relabeled Nvidia chips with a nonexistent fake brand, and classified them as generic computer parts"
Hao Global LLC "pleaded guilty to smuggling export-controlled Nvidia H100 and H200 GPUs"; others charged (allegations).
