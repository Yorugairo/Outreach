== Federal Register 2020-28101 (85 FR, 2020-12-23), BIS: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2020-12-23/html/2020-28101.htm
Effective: "December 23, 2020"
Implements "Executive Order 13936 of July 14, 2020"
Prior action: on "June 30, 2020" BIS suspended licence exceptions for Hong Kong that differed from those for China
Rationale (fetcher extract): China's actions "fundamentally undermine Hong Kong's autonomy"; risk that "sensitive U.S. technology and items will be diverted to unauthorized end uses"
(fetcher also returned "can no longer distinguish between the export of controlled items to Hong Kong and the PRC" - attribution to the FR text vs search-summary not certain; treat as PLAUSIBLE wording)

== BIS country guidance page: https://media.bis.gov/licensing/country-guidance/hong-kong-export-controls
"exports, reexports and transfers (in-country) to Hong Kong will be treated under the Export Administration Regulations (EAR) as transactions destined for the People's Republic of China"
"On December 23, 2020, pursuant to Executive Order 13936"
"Hong Kong is frequently a transshipping point to locations around the globe. Sometimes, a US export is routed through Hong Kong to avoid US export regulations."
Red flags listed: shipping to addresses other than buyer's, secretarial firms, logistics providers - "a risk of diversion contrary to the EAR."
