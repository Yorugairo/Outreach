## A. Counterfeit-relabel + export case involving Xilinx rad-tolerant ICs (DOJ, D. Conn., 2015-2016) - PLAUSIBLE here (DOJ pages returned empty to the fetcher; details from search snippets of justice.gov/fbi.gov + exportsolutionsinc.com post dated 1/31/17)
- Defendants: Xianfeng Zuo, Jiang Yan (Shenzhen), Daofu Zhang - operators of Chinese component-trading businesses.
- 2015: sought "advanced ICs made by Xilinx" with radiation tolerance "for uses in space"; military applications in missiles and surveillance satellites.
- Nov 2015: Zhang shipped "eight counterfeit ICs, each bearing a counterfeit Xilinx brand label" to the US contact - the plan was to swap fakes for the genuine parts.
- Arrested 2015-12-10, Milford, Connecticut. Sentences: Zhang 15 months (2016-07-08); Zuo 15 months (2016-11-04); Yan ~12 months (2016-12-20); $63,000 cash forfeited.
- DOJ URLs: https://www.justice.gov/usao-ct/pr/three-chinese-nationals-arrested-scheme-steal-and-illegally-export-military-grade ; https://www.justice.gov/usao-ct/pr/citizen-china-sentenced-15-months-prison-trafficking-counterfeit-computer-chips-0 ; secondary: https://www.exportsolutionsinc.com/resources/blog/three-individuals-sentenced-for-export-violations/
- Relevance: the two-way grey market - genuine controlled parts flow out, relabelled fakes flow in.

## C. BIS enforcement scale FY2025 - PLAUSIBLE (law-firm summary of the BIS FY2025 Annual Report; the BIS PDF https://www.bis.gov/media/documents/fy2025-bis-annual-report.pdf was downloaded but not text-extractable here)

  - "$324 million in civil and criminal penalties" (calendar 2025) vs ~ $16 million (2024)
  - administrative penalties $10M (2024) -> "$108 million in 2025"; criminal fines/forfeitures/restitution $6M -> "$216 million"
