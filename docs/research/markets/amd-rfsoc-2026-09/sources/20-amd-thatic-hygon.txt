== AMD Form 10-K FY2019 (accession 0000002488-20-000008), note "Equity Joint Ventures"
URL: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2488/000000248820000008/R11.htm
"In February 2016, the Company and Higon Information Technology Co., Ltd. (THATIC), a third-party Chinese entity (JV Partner), formed a joint venture comprised of two separate legal entities"
"The Company licensed certain of its intellectual property (Licensed IP) to the THATIC JV for a total of $293 million in license fees payable over several years"
"The Company also expects to receive a royalty based on the sales of the THATIC JV's products to be developed on the basis of such Licensed IP"
"In June 2019, the U.S. Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security added certain Chinese entities to the Entity List, including THATIC and the THATIC JV"
"The Company is complying with U.S. law pertaining to the Entity List designation"
2019: "the Company recognized $60 million as licensing gain associated with the Licensed IP"
2018: "$86 million of IP-related revenue upon completion of all technology milestones"
2017: "the Company recognized $52 million as licensing gain"
"The Company's receivable from the THATIC JV for the above agreements was $13 million and $18 million as of December 28, 2019 and December 29, 2018"
