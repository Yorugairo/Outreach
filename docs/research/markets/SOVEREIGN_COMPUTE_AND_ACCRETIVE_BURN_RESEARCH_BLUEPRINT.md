1. What are the definitive upstream bottlenecks in energy baseload, nuclear enrichment (HALEU), advanced semiconductor packaging, and orbital sensing?

## Verdict up front

1. **The Physical Inversion ("Bits to Atoms"):**
   The era of zero-marginal-cost software dominance is reversing. AI model scaling has hit a hard physical wall bounded by gigawatt grid interconnection queues, nuclear fuel enrichment monopolies, high-purity alloy metallurgy, and optical interconnects. Companies controlling interconnect-approved land, zero-carbon baseload power, or critical regulatory approvals occupy unprecedented economic moats.
2. **The Accretive Burn vs. Dilutive Incineration Standard:**
   Traditional backward-looking Return on Invested Capital (ROIC) metrics fail during early infrastructure build-outs. We categorize assets through two rigorous screening paths:
   - **Path A (Cash-Flow Accretion):** Cash-rich chokepoints with EBIT margin $\ge 10\%$, 3-year share dilution CAGR $\le 2\%$, Net Debt/EV $\le 10\%$, and positive operating cash flow (e.g., $INOD, $DCO, $MTRN, $ACMR).
   - **Path B (Capacity & Backlog Accretion):** Capital-intensive burners whose burn is strictly milestone-driven and contractually derisked, characterized by Backlog-to-Burn Ratio (BBR) $\ge 1.0\text{x}$, minimum 18 months cash runway, and convex multi-year off-taker agreements (e.g., $APLD, $WULF, $LEU, $OKLO, $ALOY, $AMPX).
3. **The Sovereign Baseload Chokepoint (Nuclear & Enrichment):**
   Centrus Energy ($LEU) holds the only operational domestic high-assay low-enriched uranium (HALEU) production capability in the Western Hemisphere, creating an absolute monopoly as the U.S. ban on Russian uranium takes full effect by 2028. Behind-the-meter nuclear and hydro power sites (such as TeraWulf's Lake Mariner at 430 MW and \$0.035/kWh) trade at massive discounts to replacement cost while hyperscalers compete for prompt megawatts.
4. **Integration with Content Production:**
   Every balance sheet audit, PPA power contract, and backlog-to-burn ratio in this docket serves as direct primary source material for the forensic financial explainers produced in **Money Physics** (e.g. Apex Chart Reads, balance-sheet arithmetic, and capital allocation contrast pairs).
