The operator, ruling on the alive Tokyo dock at 20 px with the parallax planes (A) beside the same picture flat under Ken
Burns (B), both over the same living water: *"i think the parallax + drift can read as random motion, whereas ken burns+ drift
gives us directionally controlled motion, which can matter for peoples perception of the story."*

**Why:** each plane of a layered plate walks at its own depth under a free idle, so the eye reads no intent; a Ken Burns lean
is one move the viewer can attribute. Liveness alone is not the goal - attributable motion is (E49's "never still" and E56's
light both serve the story's direction, not motion for its own sake).

**How to apply:** a shipping long-form row names the ALIVE FLAT plate (the generated life region over the approved still as ONE
plane) under `world.ken_burns` with the 20 px drift (shorts 30-40; the 2 px constant the floor). A layered plate is a route for
a camera move with a direction of its own (a landing, E51), never carried by the drift; its drift follows the camera or is off
(R26-164). Before proposing any plate motion ask: which direction did the story give it? Related: [nothing-ever-goes-truly-still](nothing-ever-goes-truly-still.md),
[visible-difference-and-local-life](visible-difference-and-local-life.md), [rings-retired-e56](rings-retired-e56.md), [dip-is-a-world-change](dip-is-a-world-change.md).
