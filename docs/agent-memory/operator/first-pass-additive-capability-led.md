The normal-for-which-bridge one-shot was offered for the operator's watch gate-clean and thin: pages only, one plate,
single-line charts with no comparator or reference, no sequence, 0:40-0:47 near still. The operator, from the frame
sheets alone: "doesn't feel like an improvement over prior shorts"; "we need more chart variety, better comparators,
and more on-screen action. transforms should actually lead to new charts. We don't use our stop motion capabilities,
or really most any of our capabilities."

**The definition (the operator, verbatim):** "a one-shot is never a test of the page with no library, a one-shot is
always your best attempt to produce an complete episode in a pass without my input." Every capability, plate, dock
and source is in scope from the first row.

**Why:** (1) I framed it as a test of the page (no docks/clips) and kept that scope (no docks/clips) after the operator handed it over as an episode; (2) my CD pass
fixed every defect by DELETING (figure, span, note, callout), so frames got clean and empty - I judged clean, not
good, and the gates cap errors but set no floor for richness; (3) charts were chosen from the series already on disk,
not from the comparison each claim needs; (4) no capability inventory per beat; (5) never set beside an approved cut.

**Resources are standing permission, not optional (the operator, same day):** "i had even given you permission to
send the work orders etc as needed ... you already have acces to the scml, fred data, yahoo data, and gemini research
agent via bridge. You should always make use of your resources to empower yourself." The research packet is a
starting point; comparators and news heads are fetched (FRED/Yahoo/SCML, bridge work orders to Gemini) without asking.

**How to apply:**
- Story first: every claim names its comparator (vs when / what / whom) BEFORE a series is picked; fetch it (sourced).
- Capability inventory before the shot table (CAPABILITIES.md, ANIMATION-REGISTRY): each beat picks a species or
  form; floors - stop-motion used, transforms that LAND ON A NEW CHART FORM (morph/recast), distinct species count.
- Reference parity: events/min and compositions/min vs the approved shorts (Tokyo remake, Japan) and Bravos, same
  tool (E38); below the reference is not ready.
- A review fix that removes motion names its replacement; a pass never lowers events/min.
- Put our self-watch sheet beside an approved short's sheet before offering a cut.
