Operator, reading the ep1 analytics (2026-09-03): the thumbnail converts
at 20% and traffic is ~23% search / ~28% YouTube surfaces, so packaging
is fine - "the weakness is in the script. The average drop-off happens
on the static chart being held across plates. We thought we were helping
them read the chart more, but that's our fault for assuming they want to
do homework. The chart is the proof for the narrative, not the homework
for the viewer."

**What we had backwards:** the shot table's "topic-governed exit" (E11
topic-exit audit) held Bravos' chart 0:09-0:50 across four plates and
ours 0:50-1:11 across three. Those "held-dock stills" are where the
audience left.

**STALE FIGURE, corrected 2026-09-08:** the "converts at 20%" above was an EARLY-WINDOW number inflated by warm
traffic - friends plus Facebook targeting of AI people. **Lifetime thumbnail CTR is ~8%**, about industry standard. So
"packaging is fine" was never established; the package is average and is a thing to FIX, not an asset to protect. E25's
conclusion (the weakness is the held chart) still stands on its own evidence and on the lifetime retention curve
([ep1-lifetime-curve](ep1-lifetime-curve.md)) - but do not re-cite the 20%, and treat early-window analytics on a small channel as
contaminated by warm traffic for CTR and retention alike.

**Standing rule (E25, doc 29 s9.30, gate M12):** a chart enters to prove
the sentence being spoken and leaves when it has - hold ceiling 10s, 6s
in the first minute; a chart never survives a plate change; if the
narrative returns to it, RE-ENTER it spotlit on the new datum (targeted
species) rather than hold. Exits are proof-governed, not topic-governed.
Direction over duration: a viewer who wants to study pauses.

**How to apply:** author every chart dock to one sentence; when a later
sentence needs the same chart, add a second dock row for the same asset
with a spotlight/callout on the new datum; never let a dock's exit sit
past the scene's end. The re-script (REWRITE-ORDER-G) is bound by it.
See [opening-minute-e24](opening-minute-e24.md), [screen-never-still](screen-never-still.md).
