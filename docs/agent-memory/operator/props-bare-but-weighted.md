A prop (a catalogued cutout, `dock_kind:prop`, stamped by `arrive: stamp`) is never put on a card - "We don't put the props on cards." It is bare of PAPER, not of WEIGHT: "the props should have shadow added to them to give them some depth/weight" (the operator, 2026-09-22). This amends the `dock_kind:prop` card's "no paper, border, shadow or rail".

**Why:** a bare cutout with no shadow floats flat on the page; the stamp's contact shadow switched off at settle, so the prop lost its weight once it landed. A frame where a placeholder card sat over the Fed read as "the prop stamped onto a card".

**How to apply:** a prop's resting drop shadow follows its painted alpha, cast from the engine's ONE stage light (the extruded bar's / the melt's), and the contact shadow hands over to it at settle (P69 T6b). When a test or frame shows a card near a prop, make sure the two read as separate things. Related: [throw-then-zoom-light-never-the-move](throw-then-zoom-light-never-the-move.md), [judge-the-frame-not-the-diff](judge-the-frame-not-the-diff.md).

**Amended 2026-09-22 (twice):** the soft drop-shadow v1 was rejected; then the 4.5 px hatch: "the cross hatching needs to be much tighter/finer" (fine engraving ~2 px pitch, thin lines, not a fence mesh). v1: - "The shadow doesn't look great, I think we need like cross hatch markings as shadow for texture." The shadow is ENGRAVED CROSS-HATCHING in the prop's silhouette (the plates are woodcut/engraving), one stage light (DROP -125), shared by bars (T10b). Chart CARDS: "need to use the whole card and use bigger fonts and thicker lines" - a card is rendered for its displayed size (P69 T10c), never a full page shrunk.
