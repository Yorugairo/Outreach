Operator ruling 2026-09-02: **the YouTube layer is a completely separate
layer.** Doc 29, the scene-evidence player, authored shot tables, the §9.15
wipe, woodblock world plates + evidence docks — all of that governs the
YouTube episodes, not the NotebookLM shorts.

On the shorts lane: **keep the brand essence, don't over-control
NotebookLM.** Essence = the six tokens applied to what WE add (captions,
padding, outro, hook prompts), Ken Burns 1.00→1.04, 4–6 words per caption
page, coral for the emphasis word. NotebookLM's own body — its info cards,
titles, hard cuts — is accepted as-is. Do not propose rebuilding it.

**Why:** I read the brand sheet's "Never" list as a mandate to rebuild the
NotebookLM body and route shorts through the house pipeline. The operator
corrected it: NotebookLM's value is speed; controlling it to the YouTube
standard destroys that. The shorts are a white-label surface, tuned, not
rebuilt.

**How to apply:** when a brand rule and NotebookLM's output conflict on a
short, apply the rule only to the layer we author. Related:
[notebooklm-omni-opener](notebooklm-omni-opener.md), [voice-lane-split](voice-lane-split.md),
[production-standards-universal](production-standards-universal.md) (which is the YouTube bar).
