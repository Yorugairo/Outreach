The operator, 2026-09-17, on P65/P66's first cards: "the whole idea of the recipes was that they worked"; "you literally just threw on a
card dock, then dipped. That is NOT at all the card-becomes-the-chart mechanism"; "NOTHING in the recipes say that they have to fill an
entire scene duration, or that we can't use Recipe 1 + additional effects, or even multiple recipes"; "you dropped most of the continuity
building transitions like morphs, transformation, and 'the door'"; "I don't really understand why each step was a huge regression".

**Why:** every step was built from tokens outward - a skeleton signature list I typed from memory (no door, no morph, no snap), a recipe
walker that counts members and calls it a fire, gates tightened after the approved cuts and then used to judge them, cards written from
records nobody had watched. The approved cuts' FRAMES are the reference (E38, E99 s60/s67/s69/s70); a recipe is a proven TIMING of a few
effects that composes with others inside a beat, and its length says nothing about it.

**How to apply:** before naming a vocabulary, a threshold or a mechanism list, run docs_find and cite the CAPABILITIES row and the approved
table line (recall-before-propose); measure the reference first and calibrate the gate to it, never the reverse; a fire is what a frame
shows at the instant, not what a token walk counts; nothing reaches the operator's queue that I have not read on a side-by-side sheet
against the cut it claims to reproduce; a gate row is a diagnosis of what to add after the recipe, never a reason to drop it.
