The operator, 2026-09-17, on the generated base v7 after four compiler passes had brought it to ten of eleven instants matching the
approved Tokyo cut: "Sound effects are way off, we're playing spiral and whirls when there's no spiral or whirl effect. You didn't attach
the outro either. And the charts don't have their 'life' or Bravos lessons - we changed colors to be more visible on thumbnails if you
recall. this is a decent re-build of the original, not what should be used as the new base."

**Why:** I measured the base against the cut its plan was read back from, so "the same as the approved cut" was the ceiling, and the
approved cut predates E67 (the electric inks), the Bravos lessons and s71. Matching it proved the compiler transcribes; it proved nothing
about a base. The cue map keyed cues on row tokens, not on effects that fire; the closing row held a page where the project has an outro.

**How to apply:** the read-back plan is the compiler's regression test. A base is shown on a NEW plan (the Opus one-shot's), generated
with the doctrine's defaults - E67 inks, cues derived from the compiled timeline's events, the project's outro and brand line as the
closing row, a named thing arriving where a light would have been - and read beside the doctrine, not beside an old cut. Six judgements
also landed: a dip never before a snap; scene-aware placement, never centred over a narrative plate; even pill counts; a held light that
points at nothing is noise. See [frames-inward-not-tokens-outward](frames-inward-not-tokens-outward.md), [throw-then-zoom-light-never-the-move](throw-then-zoom-light-never-the-move.md).
