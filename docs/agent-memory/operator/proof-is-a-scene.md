The planted-morph card was re-windowed to "show the scene before" and the clip opened on the golden fixture: a synthetic
lobed blob standing still on cream for four seconds, then the cut. The operator: "what you just showed me makes no sense, you
showed me a still frame with an ink splotch held for 6 seconds or something...we would never do that."

**Why:** a golden fixture proves a MECHANISM (the trace, the ground, the morph, the build) for the test suite; it is not a beat.
When the operator asks to "see the scene before", they mean a real beat as a short would play it - a real plate, a real planted
element, docks, captions, idle - not the fixture's empty hold.

**How to apply:** before serving any watch card, ask whether a viewer of the short could meet that clip. If the clip is a
fixture, build the beat in a private test-bed directory (Tokyo / Japan, never an approved cut - E45) and serve that instead.
