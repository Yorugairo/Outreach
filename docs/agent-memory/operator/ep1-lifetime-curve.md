**"The AI Bubble" IS Steel and Paper episode one** - the same video E21 and E25 were ruled on. Read again at lifetime
(operator: *"we just gave it time to breathe"*), it finally has enough views to have a shape.

**13:27 long, 6:01 average view duration, 44.7 % average percentage viewed.** The shape, not the average:

- **0:00 - ~1:30 - the bleed.** ~95 % down to the low 40s. More than half the audience leaves in ninety seconds.
- **~2:00 - ~11:00 - FLAT**, 40-48 % with only small jitter. Everyone who got past the entrance stayed.
- **~11:00 - 13:27** - a gentle fade to the mid 30s.

**It confirms the original diagnosis, on data.** E25 put the drop-off on the charts held 0:09-0:50 and 0:50-1:11; E21
measured the opening as the thinnest minute and named **0:57 as the first-drop point**. The curve's steep section is
the same window, arrived at independently. Both rulings were right, and now there is a curve behind them instead of an
inference. What the curve ADDS: the body was never the problem, so all of ep1's loss is the first ninety seconds and
E24/E25's first-minute rules (promise by 0:45, 6 s chart ceiling in the opening minute) point at exactly the right
place.

**What it cannot tell us - I got this wrong first and it matters.** I read the flat middle as proof that the engine
work (captions as motion, species, the idle, the ledger page) had fixed a mid-video sag. There is no before-and-after:
this is ONE video and it predates all of it. **This curve tests none of the engine work** - the first curve that will
is the first video shipped on it. Do not cite it as validation. And "ep1 died of visual stillness" is a compression of
[screen-never-still](screen-never-still.md) that reads as a whole-video claim; E21's own measurements were about the OPENING.

**How to apply.** Read a curve's SHAPE before its average, and name where the loss sits before deciding what to fix.
Do not diagnose a curve early - this one only became readable once views accumulated, which is what "time to breathe"
bought. **n is still small**: the shape is trustworthy, individual bumps are one or two people, never tune a dial to a
wiggle. And do not grade 44.7 % against an industry benchmark - measure the reference with the same tool
([thresholds-from-the-reference](thresholds-from-the-reference.md)); the reference now has a name, **Bravos Research**, whose searcher watched 96 % of
