The pipeline grill (2026-09-13, `docs/content-video-engine/GRILL-PIPELINE-VALUE-2026-09-13.md`) mined the approved cuts: the
thin one-shot ran 17.9 events/min (as busy as Tokyo, 3x Bravos) with 1 chart form, 0 docks, 0 chart-to-chart transforms, a
0.2:1 narrative:chart ratio and NO recurring combination (Steel's badge ladder repeats 13x; every combination in the thin cut
fired once). 12 of the 15 measured recipes never fired; 9 of those need a dock. `chart_to` (built 09-07/09-10, watched in
Tokyo's test builds) has never been in an approved cut - the only cut authored after it was mine and did not use it, after
~51 ledger lines asking for a chart to transform. 67 of 73 wired effects have never fired in an approved cut.

**The operator:** *"the individual effect is not really the key, it's blending a few of them together that creates the
effect"*; recipes + the capability/comparator plan per beat; the critic audits whether the recipe worked. Rulings E96 (floors +
recipes) and E97 (the rig as relations).

**Why:** the gates cap errors but set no floor for richness; "events per minute" would have passed the thin cut; an effect the
author never combines is an effect the viewer never sees.

**How to apply:** author a beat as a recipe (proven = a played instant in an approved cut); before offering a watch, measure:
>= 3 chart forms, >= 1 chart-to-chart transform, docks on >= 1/3 of beats, >= 60% of beats on a proven recipe, narrative:chart
>= 1:1, parity with the best approved short; use every capability from the first row. Recipes: `docs/research/runs/grill_pipeline-value/recipes_r1.md`
(15 seeds); rows R26-103..108. See [first-pass-additive-capability-led](first-pass-additive-capability-led.md), [composite-effects-keep-every-phase](composite-effects-keep-every-phase.md),
[bravos-reference](bravos-reference.md).
